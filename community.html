How you enter

There is no membership fee and no rank to buy. The way in is a week of practice, not a purchase:

  1. Join a room. Read for a few days before posting. That is normal here.
  2. Do the first week. The seven-day protocol gives one spirit per day, its correspondences, the command and a ten-minute task.
  3. Bring one result. A log entry, a doubt, a failure. That is what the group is for, and it is what makes you part of it.

One practice, one clock. The circle works with the lunar phase and the planetary hours, so the tools are timed the same way for everyone:

Rules of the circle

  • Practice first. Questions are welcome; performance is not.
  • No selling in the rooms. If you make something, ask first.
  • Disagree with the method, not the person. The tradition is a set of arguments, not a loyalty test.
  • Nothing here promises a result. We log, compare and talk. That is the whole claim.
