Astrology App Reviews: The 12 Android Apps Worth Installing

Most astrology apps are the same three features wrapped in a subscription. These are the ones that do something different, what each one costs, and the honest reason to skip the rest.

Why most astrology apps are not worth installing

The app stores are full of astrology products that offer one calculation, three free interpretations, and then a weekly subscription for everything else. The subscription is the product. The chart is the funnel. If you open an app, cast your chart, and hit a paywall before you get a single line of interpretation, you have been sold a preview.

There is a second problem: almost none of them work without a connection. A subscription app in a temple, in a ritual, or on a train is useless, because the moment you need it is exactly the moment there is no signal.

The apps that survive a year of use in this catalogue solve at least one of those two problems: they either give the whole thing away once, or they keep working with no network.
