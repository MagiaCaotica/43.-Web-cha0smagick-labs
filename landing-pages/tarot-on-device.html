What "on-device" actually means

Most apps that call themselves AI tarot send your question to a server. This one does not. The interpretation runs locally: a compact language model (MiniLM, 23 MB) compares your question with the cards in the spread and returns a reading. On phones with Google's on-device model (Gemini Nano) it goes further; when that model is not present, the app falls back to its own oracle instead of failing.

Nothing leaves the phone. There is no account to create and no history stored anywhere but your device.

What it is not

It is not a chatbot and it does not pretend to know your future. It reads the cards you drew against the question you wrote, using the deck's own symbolism. The reading is a structure for thinking, not a verdict.
