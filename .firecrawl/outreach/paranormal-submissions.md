Paranormal Daily News is an award-winning paranormal magazine. We are looking for a small team of writers who want to be a part of the magazine and write regular columns or articles on various aspects of the paranormal, parapsychology or spirituality.

The magazine will also be promoted professionally by a fully qualified content marketing company to ensure it gets maximum publicity.

As a columnist, you will become part of a focused team that is dedicated to the research of the paranormal and disseminating educational-focused content that is interesting, exciting, and most of all - educational. As a regular contributor to articles, you will have the immediate benefits of mass exposure.

## To date, PDN is accepted in all news outlets online, including Google News with a global audience.
