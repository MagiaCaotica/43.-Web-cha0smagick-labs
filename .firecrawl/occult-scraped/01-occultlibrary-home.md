> “The occult library isn’t about borrowing books—it’s about exploring, preserving, and sharing knowledge in all its forms.”

Welcome to The Occult Library—your volunteer-run, nonprofit hub for all things occult, from books and art, to movies, TV shows, podcasts, and more. We maintain an ever-expanding catalog of active and retired publishers, authors, artists, bookstores, artisans, and beyond. Please note we do not offer reading facilities; instead, our goal is to serve as a central resource for discovering and researching occult materials. We’re continuously growing and adding new features, so be sure to subscribe to our email list to stay informed and be among the first to explore our latest offerings.
