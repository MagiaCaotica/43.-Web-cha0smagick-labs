My name is Duncan Barford. I am a writer, podcaster, and psychodynamic counsellor. _OEITH_ is my website, drawing together my work and interests in magick, spirituality, and the paranormal, focusing in particular on how these intersect within personal experience, psychology, and spiritual practice.

_OEITH_ originally was a blog, which ran from 2009 to 2014, serving as an online supplement to [my book of the same title published by Aeon 2010](https://oeith.co.uk/books/#oeith). The best articles from this first phase are [now available as a book](https://oeith.co.uk/books/#emoe).

I suspended the blog when I began training as a counsellor, concerned that a conspicuous interest in the occult might prove professionally unhelpful. But having now qualified and worked for a number of years in various therapeutic contexts, I am enjoying discussing openly again my deep and lifelong interest in this field.

If you are interested in meeting with me for counselling, take at look at [my other website](http://duncanbarford.uk/) for further information and how you can get in touch.
