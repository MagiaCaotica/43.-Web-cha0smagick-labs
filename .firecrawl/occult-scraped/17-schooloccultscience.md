### What are malefic and benefic planets in astrology?

In Vedic Astrology, astrological planets are classified as malefic and benefic based on their effects on an individual's life. A malefic planet typically brings challenges and difficulties, while a benefic planet tends to bring positive outcomes and support.

Malefic Planets: These include planets like Saturn, Mars, and Rahu. Their influence can cause delays, obstacles, and hardships depending on their position in the astrology houses chart.
Benefic Planets: These include Jupiter, Venus, and Mercury, which generally bring prosperity, harmony, and growth.
The impact of these planets varies based on their planetary position in the Astrology chart and the specific zodiac sign they occupy. Understanding the roles of these planets is crucial for accurate Astrology predictions and Horoscope astrology.

In Horoscope today and for future predictions, such as Horoscope 2024, knowing the influence of malefic and benefic planets helps in providing insightful and practical guidance. ### What is Parijat yoga in astrology?

Also known as Kalpadruma yoga, this yoga is bit complex. It is formed when :

1.Lagna lord say A

2.Dispositor (sign lord where A is posited) of A say B

3.Dispositor of B say C

4.Navamsa dispositor of C say D

When above A, B, C, D are in the kendra, trines, own sign or in their exaltation signs, then this yoga is formed.

The native born with above yoga will be strong, wealthy, like a king, pious, victorious and merciful.

### What is the significance of lifeline?

The lifeline is one of the most important lines in palm lines or hast rekha. It starts from the edge of your palm, between the thumb and forefinger, and curves around the base of the thumb. Many people think the lifeline shows how long you will live, but that’s not true. Instead, the lifeline is more about your overall health, physical strength, and the events that shape your life.

In hand lines reading, a deep and long lifeline may suggest a healthy and energetic life, while a shorter or broken lifeline might indicate challenges or changes, like illness or travel.
