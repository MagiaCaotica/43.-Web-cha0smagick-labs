## Esoteric Podcast Extraordinaire

Since 2006, Occult of Personality has been the premier podcast for exploring **esoteric traditions**, **occult philosophy**, and the **Mysteries of Reality**.
Each episode features in-depth conversations with world-class guests discussing **magick**, **alchemy**, **Tarot**, **Freemasonry**, **Thelema**, **mysticism**, **symbolism**, and much more.


Whether you're interested in **spiritual philosophy**, **mystical experience**, **esoteric orders**, **grimoires**, or the **transformative power of occult symbols**,
you'll find thoughtful, insightful discussions that go beyond the surface to explore the deep currents of **esoteric spirituality**.
