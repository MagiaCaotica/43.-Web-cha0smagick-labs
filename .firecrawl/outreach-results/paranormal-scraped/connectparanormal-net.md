## [Exploring the Stone Tape Theory: Ghosts or Recordings?](https://connectparanormal.net/2026/05/18/exploring-the-stone-tape-theory-ghosts-or-recordings/)





The stone tape theory suggests that certain materials, especially stone, can absorb and replay emotional energy from past events, resulting in ghostly apparitions. Originating from late 19th-century research and popularized by Nigel Kneale’s 1972 play, it proposes a materialistic basis for hauntings and encourages scientific inquiry into paranormal experiences.
