# ['Round the Cauldron](https://roundthecauldron.com/)

life, magic, and crafts

When you search online for witchcraft or pagan content, you often find a lot of tutorials, educational information, and guides. All of that is great considering how accessible witchcraft and paganism have become in the last 30 years, especially with the rise of the internet. What you don’t see often, though, is a witch’s real life. It’s hard to find blogs and videos from a witch just detailing what they go through, what’s on their mind, and how they live their lives both as a witch and a person.

‘Round the Cauldron was born out of a desire to share knowledge. It has since grown into a way to share my life, my love, and my passions with the world. If you’ve ever been around a campfire, you’ll know how the dancing flames tend to bring out our worries, our stories, and our dreams. That’s the purpose here ’round the cauldron — I am sharing my worries, my stories, and my dreams with you.

I am more than a witch, and this is more than a witchcraft blog.

![A close-up photograph of a purple flower against large green leaves.](https://roundthecauldron.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Eggplant-Flower-rotated.jpg)

## Life

Real life is messy, unpredictable, and magical. I share stories and knowledge here ’round the cauldron, not judgment. Life is magical, and life as a magical person is worth celebrating and honoring, not mystifying. From parenting to grief, life deserves to be shared.

![A person holds a small handmade besom.](https://roundthecauldron.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/altar-besom-consecration.jpg)

## Magic

Whether you honor the world through general paganism or stick to a structured tradition, all magic is worth honoring. We are surrounded by an abundance of energy and presence every single day, even if we don’t realize it. Here ’round the cauldron, I honor that energy and weave magic from the threads of the unseen world.

![A cropped image of the upper half of a crocheted goddess doll meant to imitate the divine feminine. A soft pink heart is sewn onto the center.](https://roundthecauldron.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/blue-pink-heart-goddess-doll-square.jpg)

## Crafts

Creation and community are at the heart of the cauldron, and when we gather ’round, we share in each others’ energy. Fiber arts is the focus of my creation here ’round the cauldron. I lovingly share it with you, dear reader, in the form of free patterns, conversations, and products for purchase.

## What Now?

I welcome you to read my latest blog posts, check out my latest [YouTube video](https://www.youtube.com/@roundthecauldron), and be here in this space. _I welcome you ’round the cauldron._
