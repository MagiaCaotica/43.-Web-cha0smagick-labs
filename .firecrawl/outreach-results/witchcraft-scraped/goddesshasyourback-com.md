“I’m going to be homeless” – this thought ran like fire in my mind.

I felt so overwhelmed.

I needed the shortest possible prayer.

Here it is: “Gods, help me.”

Over and over, I would say this.

Did it give me total relief? No. Why? I deal with depression symptoms. What does that mean? Often, I feel like crap!

But saying “Gods help me” truly works.

How? It reminds me of important things.

Here are some important details:

- You’re not alone
- The God and Goddess care about you.
- You have hope that things can improve.
- A solution is possible—at any moment.
- The Goddess can open for you, a new possibility and a new opportunity.

These important details can give you some moments of relief.

May these insights support your path.

“When are you calm during the day?” a friend, Alicia, asked me.

“Usually when I first wake up in the morning. Before the weight of the day crashes down on my shoulders,” I replied.

Alicia wasn’t expecting that answer.

Meanwhile…

Lidia, someone I know, works in retail. She’s noticing that more and more customers in recent weeks have been saying rude, impatient things. Downright mean insults.

Lidia needs to stay calm – or lose her job.

I asked her, “Do you have some way to hold your calm?”

Lidia said, “Every day, I have a 3-minute ritual in which I purposely shift to calm.”

_“Each of us must find our ideal way to attune with deity.” – Scott Cunningham_

Here is a ritual you can use.

**Own Your Calm Ritual – Invite Goddess**

Light a white candle and say these words:

_Goddess,_

_As pure as light, may Your calm wash over me._

_May I bathe in this candle’s blessed light._

_Thank You for peace, calm, and strength._

_So Mote It Be!_

May these insights support you.

I was concerned. I had just a week to create my [400th Podcast episode](https://askawitchnow.podbean.com/e/special-400th-podcast-episode-a-gift-for-you-%e2%80%93-meditation-for-strength-and-release-from-anger-and-frustration/). **Do you feel squeezed by other people’s expectations?**

**Are you harsh with yourself about expectations?**

I was wracked with questions:

- Is my 400th Podcast Episode going to be good enough? (Eventually my team came up with this title of [Meditation for Strength and Release from Anger and Frustration](https://askawitchnow.podbean.com/e/special-400th-podcast-episode-a-gift-for-you-%e2%80%93-meditation-for-strength-and-release-from-anger-and-frustration/))
- Will it help enough people?
- Does it honor my team’s efforts to serve people through 399 episodes of my podcast?

On some lists ( [https://podcast.feedspot.com/wicca\_podcasts/](https://podcast.feedspot.com/wicca_podcasts/)) my podcast is designated as the #3 Wicca Podcast.

**_Can I live up to that?_**

Whoa. STOP.

**What would Goddess tell me?**

I paused for quiet time.

I realized…

- It’s not about the milestone.
- It is about showing up—to do a service for my community.

As we did the episode, it went _better_ than we expected.

It showed up as an inspiration.

**We showed up in the Moment.**

Do you get caught up in expectations and worries?

Imagine what the Goddess would tell you.

Ask:

- Goddess, how do I navigate the expectations and worries—in a healthy way?

Invite the Goddess:

- Goddess, guide me to make the best choices in this situation.

May these insights support your path.

Ever move too fast at work and bump your hand … perhaps, you _SMACK_ your hand on the corner of a desktop computer?

Owww!

One of my spiritual elders developed her own ritual.

After bumping her hand, she went to the employee restroom. She ran the water faucet. Cool water cascaded on her hand.

In her mind, she said,

_God, Goddess,_

_May this water wash away my discomfort._

She _did_ feel better.

Would you feel better to have your own brief ritual to support yourself? And would you ask the God and the Goddess for comfort?

May this support your path.

_“Keep pure your highest ideals. Strive ever towards it. Let none stop you or turn you aside.” – The Goddess speaking in_ The Charge of the Goddess _, written by Doreen Valiente_

Consider this breath ritual.

### **The Goddess Breath Ritual**

_Breathe in as you recite in your mind: “Goddess”_

_Breathe out as you recite in your mind: “Stress Away.”_

Do this cycle three times. (Yes, 3 is a sacred number.)

May this process support you on your path.
