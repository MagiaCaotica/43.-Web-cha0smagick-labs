Hi folks! Just a brief update about some online course we’ve put up for Summer 2026.

It’s time to book your trip to witchy and magical summer camp! We’ve got a few courses coming up that may be of interest to those of you with a yen for folk magic. We’ll be offering one of our multi-session classes on cartomancy as well as a few one-off classes on specific folk magical topics. We’re going to be offering the following classes over the course of July and August:

[**54 Devils – July 2026 Cartomancy Class (July 12th, 19th, and 26th)**](https://www.patreon.com/newworldwitchery/posts/54-devils-class-161249370?utm_medium=clipboard_copy&utm_source=copyLink&utm_campaign=postshare_creator&utm_content=join_link).  This is a three-session course that teaches you the lore and practice of using playing cards for divination. You get access to the live sessions, video recordings, online classroom, bonus materials, and more! ($64 for non-Patreon members)

[**Wicked Games: Occult Games and Supernatural Play**](https://www.patreon.com/newworldwitchery/posts/wicked-games-and-161258376?utm_medium=clipboard_copy&utm_source=copyLink&utm_campaign=postshare_creator&utm_content=join_link)(Wed. July 1st at 8pm). This one-session class looks at the history and lore of supernatural games as we explore divination games from the past five centuries. Includes live session and online classroom access, downloadable slides, and more! ($13 for non-Patreon members)

[**Killing the Moon: Witchcraft Initiations in Folklore**](https://www.patreon.com/newworldwitchery/posts/killing-moon-in-161258983?utm_medium=clipboard_copy&utm_source=copyLink&utm_campaign=postshare_creator&utm_content=join_link)(Wed. July 15th at 8pm). We look to folklore to see what makes a witch become a witch, including rituals, tools, familiars, and more.  Includes live session and online classroom access, downloadable slides, and more! ($13 for non-Patreon members)

[**A Dimpled Chin and the Devil Within: Magical Body Lore**](https://www.patreon.com/newworldwitchery/posts/dimpled-chin-and-161259533?utm_medium=clipboard_copy&utm_source=copyLink&utm_campaign=postshare_creator&utm_content=join_link)(Wed. August 5th at 8pm). We’ll look to the human body for its many magical applications, including divining by body markings, folk palmistry, the lore of bodily fluids, and even corpse lore.  Includes live session and online classroom access, downloadable slides, and more! ($13 for non-Patreon members)

The classes all run about 90 minutes long with time for Q&A at the end of each session. Those who attend live can participate in the in-class Q&A, but even if you can’t make the date you get the video recording and online classroom access to ask your questions there. Plus each course comes with downloadable bonus materials like slides or PDFs to help you learn.

Bonus! I’ll also be presenting on the folklore and history of [Urban Legends at the upcoming Profs & Pints at Fait La Force in Nashville](https://events.ticketleap.com/tickets/profsandpints/nashville-urban-legends) if you want a little extra summer camp fun! Tickets are on sale now for $13.50 online or $15 at the door.

The 3-session cartomancy course is $64, and the single-session courses are all $13 each. You can purchase them by following the link in our bio and clicking on “Classes, Workshops, and Events” or find them on our Patreon page at [patreon.com/newworldwitchery](http://patreon.com/newworldwitchery).

Looking forward to sharing folk magic with you soon!

-Cory
