### [Moon Books Podcast: Celebrating Lughnasadh](http://www.badwitch.co.uk/2026/07/moon-books-podcast-celebrating-lammas.html)

This weekend I'll be celebrating Lammas, or the first fruits of the harvest. I'll probably pick blackberries from my garden and make a crumble or maybe berry flapjacks. Yum.  Otherwise known as Lughnasadh, Lammas is the first harvest festival in the Modern Pagan Wheel of the Year, and takes place at the start of August.

On a recent Moon Books podcast, Rachel Patterson chatted with Jessica Howard, Katie Ness, and me - Lucya Starza - about how we work with the magic of the season.

We discuss baking bread, making corn dollies, river offerings, foraging, journaling, working with deities, and simple seasonal spells, as well as a few personal stories. We also talk about how changes in the seasons and climate can affect traditional celebrations, why gratitude matters more than obligation, and how it's fine if your practice is different from others.

Even for non-Pagans, this can be a time to pause, notice what has grown, be grateful for what you have, and get ready for the slow shift toward autumn. This conversation offers some practical ideas, as well as fun and laughter.

### [Authors On Their Writing: Feminist Witch Susan Harper](http://www.badwitch.co.uk/2026/07/authors-on-their-writing-feminist-witch.html)

[![](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEgxHetXUrP2Nh5F-ugr8c07AyZo-m91HIu0eVD7G4cRzMHZjLddv-ZigCnq_ByUItArtKZZJ8djJlcN_hhOSi7Cgs4xZDk6kxHfRrZRtSou58HAXCBrs9JYP9O3CESh5Bwm1fVhO0wPeHLNqCv2MoCZqQiqb-sBgrYcE9I5iV6sWZahqRZyglMf2YN58IU0/w130-h200/feminist%20witchcraft%20cover.jpg)](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEgxHetXUrP2Nh5F-ugr8c07AyZo-m91HIu0eVD7G4cRzMHZjLddv-ZigCnq_ByUItArtKZZJ8djJlcN_hhOSi7Cgs4xZDk6kxHfRrZRtSou58HAXCBrs9JYP9O3CESh5Bwm1fVhO0wPeHLNqCv2MoCZqQiqb-sBgrYcE9I5iV6sWZahqRZyglMf2YN58IU0/s612/feminist%20witchcraft%20cover.jpg)

I've chatted to witch, cultural anthropologist, and author Susan Harper about her work, continuing my interview series Authors on Their Own Writing:

**Q: Out of all that you've written, which is your favourite published book?**

A: That's a tough one! But it's probably _Feminist Witchcraft_, from Moon Books. I write for both popular audiences and academic audiences, so if I get to choose my favourite academic work alongside _Feminist Witchcraft_, it's the book I recently published with my writing partner, _Doing Ethnography_.

**Q: What’s _Feminist Witchcraft_ about?**

A: The book is meant to be an introduction to feminism-centered witchcraft as I practice it, and as it has grown from the Women's Spirituality of the 1970s and 1980s. It's a short little book, since it's in the Pagan Portals series, and is meant to give readers a taste of what feminist witchcraft can look like and how they can begin to build a practice.

**Q: Why is it your favourite?**

[![](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEjIzG5n-C3I08yGKSGyPlbd7C_vwmfJLrsfy-nTJx7vIo6FhivwdB0nfxTV72lFmrJ2yMDGNG0qXM5b7EcP8s6YmeX6qBljzrGwGblmEBwyR54Sfw2hyphenhyphenigGL8o8nneeFk5opH_ImK5shVcRnk6aJqQqlyB2J-vR7fmWooic792TS8-wHbk2PDJEFduTAEAL/w131-h200/Doing%20Ethnography.jpg)](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEjIzG5n-C3I08yGKSGyPlbd7C_vwmfJLrsfy-nTJx7vIo6FhivwdB0nfxTV72lFmrJ2yMDGNG0qXM5b7EcP8s6YmeX6qBljzrGwGblmEBwyR54Sfw2hyphenhyphenigGL8o8nneeFk5opH_ImK5shVcRnk6aJqQqlyB2J-vR7fmWooic792TS8-wHbk2PDJEFduTAEAL/s918/Doing%20Ethnography.jpg)

A: It's the book I wish I had had in my 20s. I had dreamed about writing a book about witchcraft for three decades, so to see it come to life was quite something. It's also my favourite because it's my attempt to be a good ancestor -- many of the writers who founded feminist witchcraft and whose work shaped me have turned out to hold trans-exclusionary ideologies and to act in ways that are harmful to Black, Brown, and Indigenous women, and this broke my heart because I believe that these attitudes are antithetical to the feminist project. I have met many folks who are skeptical of feminist witchcraft spaces because of the reputation they have due to these public figures. My philosophy has always been that the best way to navigate having ancestors who were not great is to endeavor to be better ancestors for the next generation. _Feminist Witchcraft_ is my way of doing that, by offering a vision of feminist witchcraft that rejects these narrow views and offers a path to empowerment for anyone who wants to walk it.

**Q: Tell me a bit more about yourself and anything else you’ve written.**

[![](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEiUO3hC4f1m-WksW0Z-tfJYQPmMGB8_eN5cIPGzCvU5sIl07LbIqfu2botkfdeY7pmJRQ-mO54-gLgq3zaCwFg7nPgdE-Lrlqr5_hRbIUhQXU-zbroIWLZFlfAJa2G3lt5jHJNfmNBdQjjgbyrkIGlVx7jWSy8VINIFcYdArzOuMWu0IKAcWwkwCw1s9ZRw/w133-h200/Monstrous-Mothers.jpg)](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEiUO3hC4f1m-WksW0Z-tfJYQPmMGB8_eN5cIPGzCvU5sIl07LbIqfu2botkfdeY7pmJRQ-mO54-gLgq3zaCwFg7nPgdE-Lrlqr5_hRbIUhQXU-zbroIWLZFlfAJa2G3lt5jHJNfmNBdQjjgbyrkIGlVx7jWSy8VINIFcYdArzOuMWu0IKAcWwkwCw1s9ZRw/s1200/Monstrous-Mothers.jpg)

A: I've been practicing witchcraft in various forms since the mid-1990s, and I am also a scholar of Contemporary Paganism. My doctoral dissertation was entitled _Navigating Gender Identity and Social Identity in an American NeoPagan Community_ and examined the ways in which Pagans in Texas found their paths and built community. I still write for an academic audience about Paganism and a variety of other topics, including pop culture, social science methods, reproductive justice, and queer identity and issues. I've been reading tarot for my entire adult life and that is another area in which I do research and offer workshops. I work in higher education at a large public university in the US Midwest. I'm involved with increasing college access through two large scholarship programs principally. When I'm not working or writing, I love to garden, offer workshops on tarot and witchcraft both online and in my local community, crocheting, spending time with my wife and my three cats, and spending time on the beautiful prairies where I live.

I've written quite a few things, including essays in three Moon Books anthologies -- _Naming the Goddess, The Goddess in America_, and _Seven Ages of the Goddess_. My favourite among those is probably the piece I wrote for _The Goddess in America_, "The Goddess and the Feminist." It holds the seeds of what became _Feminist Witchcraft_. I've published two scholarly articles on Pagan topics -- "All Cool Women Should Be Bisexual": Female Bisexual Identity in an American NeoPagan Community (Journal of Bisexuality, 2010) which also appears in an an edited volume, _Sexuality, Religion, and the Sacred_; and "In the Service of All LIfe: Towards a Contemporary Pagan Ethics of Abortion" (Gender and Religion, 2022). My writing partner and I published _Doing Ethnography_, a guide to doing ethnographic research (the signature method of anthropology and sociology) last year (Guilford, 2025). We also published a chapter in an anthology, Monstrous Mothers (Demeter Press, 2023) -- our chapter is called "Science Put Babies in My Belly: Motherhood and Monstrosity in Orphan Black."

[![](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEhpzI5JLe2RxuryB5NS4ZqFGhwopSO9nhNpKW9NKI6T_zO-4bM44Wa3WcZtu92grkcokLiJsurMAxTBs8ODugjmpdSX_iB1CiuoE9ucIC5Kb3J0Io2lVTS5NK4i_ORIA0va7Kew7L_x6irFTIWuzwaaqM8KjNqjjUQYXKeRBDCDLIK7SPAqncaf3Av5KC1o/w133-h200/Pensive%20Reader%20Antique.jpg)](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEhpzI5JLe2RxuryB5NS4ZqFGhwopSO9nhNpKW9NKI6T_zO-4bM44Wa3WcZtu92grkcokLiJsurMAxTBs8ODugjmpdSX_iB1CiuoE9ucIC5Kb3J0Io2lVTS5NK4i_ORIA0va7Kew7L_x6irFTIWuzwaaqM8KjNqjjUQYXKeRBDCDLIK7SPAqncaf3Av5KC1o/s1800/Pensive%20Reader%20Antique.jpg)

**Q: What are you working on now?**

I'm working on three major things right now. The first, with my writing partner, is a chapter for an edited volume on the Witch in Popular Culture titled _They Can't Burn Us All_. Our contribution is titled "The Revolution Will Not Be Instagrammed: Witchcraft, Wellness, and Commodification in the 21st Century" and looks at the way in which witchcraft is repositioned as self-care or wellness and as something you can purchase. My two larger projects are a volume for Moon Books' Practical History of Pagan Britain series about the 19th century occult movement -- I joking call it the Weird Victorian Guys book, and it's just fascinating really digging into the history and focusing on Britain since I typically work in the US. The second is an outgrowth of the article I referenced above about Pagan ethics of abortion -- Bloomsbury asked me to turn that article into a book length  monograph, which was a bit heady! It focuses on US Pagans and their ethics about abortion, something that there's not a lot of other writing about.

### [Review: Hecate, Goddess of Magick & Witchcraft](http://www.badwitch.co.uk/2026/07/review-hecate-goddess-of-magick.html)

[![](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEgxja-3Hm5PF9_0gABOuqgfQlAWf_0RsREejfOtfZS3YujJ8EG6cI-CpTHTnDgNG88gIcJyvxwRqHMrla0Req5a78BtkBxKZSlkjkOP8H_mi2tPckIqdj2HhVd_CYnltuBx1i65scPYmShKyUjjIhHFXyNhSDU6NnrQ47nwcwGU9GwDuIc7Bnh0dLaQLFeq/w130-h200/hecate.jpg)](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEgxja-3Hm5PF9_0gABOuqgfQlAWf_0RsREejfOtfZS3YujJ8EG6cI-CpTHTnDgNG88gIcJyvxwRqHMrla0Req5a78BtkBxKZSlkjkOP8H_mi2tPckIqdj2HhVd_CYnltuBx1i65scPYmShKyUjjIhHFXyNhSDU6NnrQ47nwcwGU9GwDuIc7Bnh0dLaQLFeq/s612/hecate.jpg)

Hecate is the goddess of the crossroads. She stands with torches to light the way in darkness. As Irisanya Moon writes in _Pagan Portals - Hecate, Goddess of Magick and Witchcraft_, she's thought of as being among the witchiest  of goddesses, and is invoked by the "weird sisters" in Shakespeare's Macbeth.

I read _Pagan Portals - Hecate_ because I always enjoy Irisanya Moon's books. She has a lovely style of writing and offers accurate historical research as well as practical ways of working with goddesses as modern pagans while referring to ancient practice too. However, I soon realised that this is the book I needed to read now, and Hecate is the goddess I need to work with because I'm at a crossroads in my own life.

Next month - Augtust 2026 - I will start receiving my state pension. I could, technically, retire, but I'm not in a standard job. I'm a freelance writer and teacher of magical workshops. I love doing those things, but writing and teaching have never earnt me huge amounts of money. I've had to be frugal and sometimes take on paid work because I desparately needed the cash even though I would rather have spent the time doing other things. My state pension will give me a bit more financial security.

I have no desire to stop writing, but I can afford to be more choosy about what I write. Writing a book is a big commitment. Part of my crossroads means deciding whether to propose another Pagan Portals book or concentrate on another novel following on from my first that was published, _[Erosion](https://www.collectiveinkbooks.com/moon-books/our-books/erosion-novel)_.  A practical magic book would sell more copies, but fiction writing is where my heart lies.

Hecate is the right goddess to ask for help in choosing the path forwards. Inspired by Irisanya's book, I'll be lighting a candle, scrying, and asking the help of the goddess to show the way.

But, that's enough of my own life story. Here's the synopsis of _Pagan Portals - Hecate_ from publisher [Moon Books' website](https://www.collectiveinkbooks.com/moon-books/our-books/pagan-portals-hecate-goddess-magick-witchcraft):

> "Hecate waits for us at the crossroads, torches in each hand. The goddess of land, sea, and sky is wise and wondrous, terrifying and holy. Triple-faced Hecate does not tell us the way; she shows us what can happen when we step into our power. With practices, trances, spells, and immersive exercises, you can build a relationship with Hecate: light-bringer, gate-breaker, and star-walker. The Goddess of Magick and Witchcraft inspires, guides, and grants safe passage in the darkest moments."

**About the author**

Irisanya Moon is a writer, witch, teacher, poet, and Reclaiming initiate who has practiced magick for 20-plus years. She blends grounded, graceful, and radically authentic facilitation to inspire transformation and liberation at the personal and collective levels. Her previous books include Pagan Portals titles on _Reclaiming Witchcraft, Aphrodite, Iris, Norns, Artemis, Circe, Hestia_,  and _The Muses_.

You can [view _Pagan Portals - Hecate_ on Amazon](https://amzn.to/3RnqOOz).
