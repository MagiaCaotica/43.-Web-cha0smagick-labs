## Pin Design Specs

- **Size**: 1000×1500 px (2:3 ratio) — standard vertical pin
- **Format**: PNG (JPEG for photos)
- **Brand colors**: Black (#000), DarkPurple (#1a0033), Gold (#c9a84c), White (#fff)
- **Style**: Dark aesthetic, occult typography, minimal text overlay, high contrast
- **Text overlay**: Title (max 6 words) in bold white/gold on dark background
- **CTAs**: "Read more →", "Try it free", "Download now"
- **Logo watermark**: Small cha0smagicklabs.com logo in corner

## Pin Categories

### 1. Article Pins (80%)
Pin blog articles with engaging titles. Rich Pins auto-sync metadata.
- Pin title: Article headline
- Description: 2-3 lines + "🔮 Read the full guide at cha0smagicklabs.com"
- Link: Direct to article URL

### 2. Product Pins (15%)
Showcase apps and books with "get it on Google Play" overlay.
- Use app screenshots + brand frame
- Link: Direct to app page on site (pages/app-details.html?id=X)

### 3. Video Pins (5%)
Short occult demos (app walkthroughs, sigil animations).

## Publishing Cadence

- **Daily**: 3-5 pins (mix of article + product)
- **Boards**: Distribute evenly — don't flood one board
- **Best times**: 8-11 PM EST (occult audience active late)
- **Seasonal**: Tie to astronomical events (full moon, solstices, eclipses)
