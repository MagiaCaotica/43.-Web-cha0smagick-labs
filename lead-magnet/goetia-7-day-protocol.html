Why a protocol and not a list

A list of 72 names is reference material. A protocol is what turns it into practice: a fixed aim written before the ritual, the planetary window recorded, the command said once, and a log entry after. After a week you have seven comparable entries instead of seven impressions.

After the seventh day, bring the log to the circle: the coven group is where a working gets discussed instead of performed for an audience.

The protocol is designed to be used next to Arcana Goetia, which holds the seal and the hour for each spirit and keeps the log on your phone. It works with a printed grimoire too; the app just removes the arithmetic.
