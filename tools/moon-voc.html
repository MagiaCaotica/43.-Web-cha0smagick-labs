What exactly is "Void of Course"?
The Moon is "Void of Course" from the moment it makes its last major Ptolemaic aspect (conjunction, sextile, square, trine, opposition) to any planet until it ingresses into the next zodiac sign. Traditional astrology considers this a "dead zone" — initiatives started during VOC rarely complete as intended. Best for: routine tasks, introspection, banishing. Avoid: launches, contracts, surgeries, major rituals.
