Does the birth time matter?

Yes. The birth time can change the approximate distribution significantly.

What if I do not know my time?

Does this tool work offline?

Yes. The whole calculation happens in your browser, so the tool keeps working offline and never sends your data to a server.

About this tool

Zodiac Cusp Calculator is a free tool from Cha0smagick Labs for occultists, astrologers and chaos magicians. It runs in your browser with no signup and no download.
