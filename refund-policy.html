Refund Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026. This policy covers the two places where Cha0smagick Labs sells digital products: PDF books on Hotmart and Android apps on Google Play. It is the real policy, not a draft.

The short version

PDF books bought on Hotmart

You have 7 calendar days from the date of purchase to request a refund. Hotmart processes the payment and the refund; we cannot refund from our side, but we will help you through the process.

  • How to request it: open the purchase in your Hotmart account and use the refund option, or write to magiacaoticapractica@gmail.com with the order reference and the email you bought with.
  • You do not have to justify it. A refund request inside the 7 days is not an argument you need to win.
  • What happens after: the download link stops working and any access through Hotmart ends. Delete the files you had already downloaded.
  • When the money comes back: Hotmart decides the approval and your bank or card issuer decides how long it takes to show. That usually takes a few business days, and it is outside our control.

Android apps bought on Google Play

Google charges and refunds these purchases, under Google Play's own refund rules:

  • Within 48 hours: you can request the refund directly in Google Play and it is handled by Google, not by us.
  • After 48 hours: Google will point you to the developer. Write to us with the order number and what is wrong (for example, the app does not run on your device) and we will review it case by case.
  • What happens after: a refunded app is uninstalled and you lose access to it. Google allows one refund per app: if you buy it again, the second purchase cannot be refunded.

What this policy does not cover

  • Change of mind after the window. Once the 7 days (Hotmart) or the 48 hours (Google Play) have passed, a refund is no longer automatic and depends on the case.
  • "It did not give me results." No product on this site promises a spiritual, medical, financial or supernatural outcome. That is stated on every book page and in the disclaimer.
  • Requests we cannot verify. We need a transaction reference and the purchase email to find the order. Requests without them cannot be processed.

Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy removes rights you have by law as a consumer, including the rules for digital content in your country. Where the law gives you a longer period than the windows above, the law wins. Hotmart and Google Play may also offer their own processes and deadlines, and those apply to the payment itself.

How to contact us

Email magiacaoticapractica@gmail.com with: the product name, the order or transaction reference, the platform (Hotmart or Google Play), the purchase date and what happened. We reply as soon as we can, normally within two business days.

Do not send card numbers, passwords or authentication codes by email. We never need them.

Changes to this policy

If the refund windows or the platforms change, this page will be updated and the date at the top changed. The version that applies to your purchase is the one published on the day you bought.
