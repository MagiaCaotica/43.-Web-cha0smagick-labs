What a birth chart app should actually give you

Most of them give you the Sun sign and hold the rest. You tap on the app, you get a glyph, you get a paragraph about being creative, and the moment you ask what your Moon sign does the app asks for a subscription.

A real natal chart is not a Sun sign. It is a wheel of about thirty positions: Sun, Moon and rising sign, the personal planets, the angles, the houses, and every aspect formed between them. Two people born in the same month can have completely different charts, and the differences between them are where the actual information is.

What you need from an app, then, is the whole wheel, the aspects drawn as patterns rather than listed as noise, the meanings attached to each placement, and a way to see what is moving now. That is the list this app is judged against.

Mean positions, VSOP87, and why the difference is visible

  • Las efemerides se calculan de dos formas. Las posiciones medias usan orbitas circulares promediadas, rapidas, con error de hasta un grado. VSOP87 integra la teoria planetaria completa y baja de esa a centimas de grado.
  • Un grado no parece nada hasta que te fijas en que el zodiaco son 30 grados por signo. Un grado es doce minutos de tiempo. La conclusion es distinta.
  • El caso peor es el Ascendente: se mueve un grado cada cuatro minutos de tiempo. Un error de dos grados son ocho minutos de reloj, y en un borde de signo eso es un signo entero equivocado.
  • En una carta con planetas cerca de los limites, la diferencia se ve. En una carta con planetas bien puestos, ninguna de las dos te va a dar una respuesta diferente. Ahi la decision no importa, y conviene decirlo claro para no vender humo.

A complete mathematical theory of planetary motion published by the Paris Observatory in 1988. Astrology programs that use it produce positions accurate to fractions of a degree instead of to a degree.
