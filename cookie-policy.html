Cookie Policy

Working draft — pending review by the legal owner. Last updated: September 24, 2026.

This page explains the cookie and similar-storage choices used by this site. It is a draft description and does not replace the notices supplied by a third-party platform.

What cookies are

Cookies are small values stored by a browser. Similar technologies can include local storage, pixels, and SDKs used by payment, translation, or measurement providers. Their purpose depends on the service that sets them.

Analytics and consent

Google Analytics 4 is configured with analytics storage denied until a visitor chooses to accept analytics. If consent is declined, analytics collection remains disabled where the consent implementation is working correctly. You can revisit your choice by clearing site data or using browser controls.

Other services

  • Google Translate may store language-preference data when the translation widget is used.
  • Hotmart or Google Play may set cookies or use platform storage when you open a checkout or store page.
  • Giscus may set storage needed to display comments.
  • Maps, fonts, and other embedded services can receive technical request data when their assets load.

Your choices

Use the Accept or Decline control when the banner appears, configure your browser to block or delete cookies, or contact us at magiacaoticapractica@gmail.com with a question. Browser settings can affect site features.

Vendor responsibility

Each provider has its own privacy notice and retention practices. We are not able to change the legal terms of Google, Hotmart, Google Play, Giscus, or another independent provider. Their notices should be reviewed before relying on a service.
