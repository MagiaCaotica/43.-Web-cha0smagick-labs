What is The Complete Practical Treatise on Magical Servitors?

Our longest and most technical title: 368 pages, 8 parts, 35 chapters, 211 figures and 6 appendices, covering the full lifecycle of a magical servitor from creation through maintenance and monitoring to banishing and failure recovery. It documents a practice without asserting that the practice works, and labels every mechanism statement as an operational, empirical or interpretive claim. Safety is treated as doctrine, with 17 contraindications, a five-level emergency protocol, a taxonomy of drift and an automatic self-dissolving servitor. The bibliography is 227 sources, each with a DOI or Wikidata identifier, audited in Appendix D. It costs $9.99 USD and exists in English and Spanish.

It costs $9.99 USD in PDF and exists in two versions, English and Spanish, with the same content. Both versions are linked from the book's page.
