What is a sigil?

A sigil is a symbol created from a written intention. It compresses a desire into a visual form that bypasses the conscious mind and speaks to the subconscious. The technique was formalized by Austin Osman Spare in the early 1900s, but the principle — symbol → subconscious → manifestation — appears in every magical tradition.

Sigils are the most accessible form of magic: no tools, no rituals, no pantheon. Just intention, a symbol, and a moment of focused will.

How to create a sigil (step by step)

  1. Write your intention as a single sentence in the present tense: "I AM CONFIDENT IN SOCIAL SITUATIONS."
  2. Remove duplicate letters — keep only the first occurrence of each letter.
  3. Arrange the remaining letters into a unique, abstract symbol. There are no rules — let your hand move freely.
  4. Simplify — remove unnecessary lines, merge shapes, make it clean.
  5. Charge it (see below).
  6. Forget it. The sigil works while you are not thinking about it.

How to charge a sigil (gnosis)

Charging means projecting your intention into the sigil with enough emotional energy that it bypasses the conscious filter. Two methods:

  • Inhibitory gnosis: deep meditation, sensory deprivation, exhaustion. The mind goes blank; you project the sigil into the void.
  • Excitatory gnosis: intense emotion, physical exertion, sensory overload. The mind is flooded; you project the sigil through the noise.

Practical charging methods: orgasm, exhaustion, breathwork, cold exposure, dancing, drumming. Choose what fits your temperament. The key is reaching a state where the conscious mind is temporarily disabled.

The 3 rules of sigil magic

  1. One intention per sigil. "I want money AND love AND health" produces a confused symbol. Pick one, sharpen it, charge it.
  2. Forget it after charging. The sigil works while you are not thinking about it. Obsessing over the outcome is the #1 reason sigils fail.
  3. Charge with genuine emotion. A sigil charged while bored does nothing. The energy must come from real desire.

Common mistakes

  • Too many sigils at once: charging 5 sigils in one session dilutes the energy. One at a time.
  • Not forgetting: checking "did it work?" every day kills the sigil. Put it away and move on.
  • Vague intentions: "I want to be happy" is too broad. "I feel confident presenting to my team on Friday" is specific and actionable.
  • No feedback loop: record results. Use the sigil intention encoder to track outcomes.

Sigil magic for money, love, protection and more

Sigils can be used for any intention: wealth, love, health, protection, creativity, academic success, career advancement. The method is the same; only the intention changes. The sigil intention encoder helps you formulate precise intentions for any goal.

For money sigils specifically: be specific about the amount and the source. "I receive $500 from an unexpected source" works better than "I am rich."
