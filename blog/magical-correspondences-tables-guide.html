The Principle: Sympathy as Operational Architecture

Magical correspondences are not poetic metaphors — they are operational architecture. The Law of Sympathy states: Things which share qualities are connected. The Sun and gold share the quality of radiance. The Moon and silver share receptivity. Mars and iron share penetrating hardness. When you assemble elements sharing correspondences with your intention, you create a resonant field that amplifies the probability of your intended outcome.

This is not metaphor. It's pattern matching across domains. The planetary hour of Mars, the color red, the metal iron, the herb nettle, the stone bloodstone, the tarot Tower, the Hebrew letter Peh — these all share the Mars pattern. When you align them simultaneously, you create constructive interference across multiple domains: temporal (planetary hour), chromatic (color), material (metal), botanical (herb), mineral (stone), archetypal (tarot). The combined field has higher signal-to-noise than any single element.

The Operational Principle: A correspondence table is a timing and materials specification sheet for ritual. It tells you: WHEN (planetary hour), WHAT COLOR, WHAT METAL, WHAT INCENSE, WHAT HERB, WHAT STONE, WHAT TAROT, WHAT HEBREW LETTER, WHAT NUMBER, WHAT NOTE, WHAT DIRECTION, WHAT BODY PART, WHAT DEITY. The table is your ritual specification sheet.

The Correspondence Equation: Ritual Power = Σ(Domain Resonance) × Timing Precision × Operator Clarity. Each aligned correspondence adds constructive interference. Timing (planetary hour) is the carrier wave. Operator clarity is the signal. Correspondences are the antenna array.

Table Construction Protocol: Building Operational Tables

Do not copy tables blindly. Build them. The act of building encodes the correspondences into your neural architecture.

The Table Construction Protocol (Per Table)

  1. Define the Intention Domain: What magical domain? Planetary, elemental, zodiacal, Goetic, Enochian, numerological, herbal, mineral, color, sound, directional, temporal.
  2. Select the Primary Axis: The organizing principle (rows): planetary (7), elemental (4/5), zodiacal (12), sephirotic (10), Enochian (30 Aethyrs), Goetic (72), numerological (9/22), herbal (100+), mineral (50+).

Map the Correspondence Columns (Minimum 8): For each row, define: Planetary Hour, Color, Metal, Incense/Resin, Herb/Plant, Stone/Crystal, Tarot Trump, Hebrew Letter, Goetic Spirit, Enochian Call, Numerological Value, Musical Note, Direction, Body Part, Deity/Spirit. Cross-Referencing Protocol: Building Resonant Fields

A single correspondence table is a line. Cross-referencing builds the resonant field — the multidimensional array that creates constructive interference.

The Cross-Referencing Protocol (Per Ritual)

  1. Identify the Intention: "I intend to [specific outcome]."
  2. Select the Ruling Principle: Which planet/element/zodiac rules this intention? (e.g., wealth → Jupiter/Sagittarius/Jupiter hour).
  3. Pull the Primary Row: From the ruling table, pull the full row for the ruler.
  4. Cross-Reference Across Tables: For each column in the primary row, pull the corresponding row from the target table.
    • Color → Color/Chakra table
    • Metal → Mineral table
    • Herb → Herbal table
    • Stone → Mineral table
    • Tarot Trump → Tarot table
    • Hebrew Letter → Sephirotic table
    • Numerological Value → Numerological table
    • Planetary Hour → Temporal table
    • Deity/Spirit → Entity Cross-Reference table
  5. Assemble the Resonant Field: Gather all corresponding materials. The field = simultaneous activation of all correspondences.

How do I use correspondence tables in actual ritual?

1) Identify your intention. 2) Find the ruling planet/element for that intention. 3) Look up the row: planetary hour, color, metal, incense, herb, stone. 4) Assemble materials matching correspondences. 5) Perform ritual during planetary hour. 6) Charge sigil/talisman using correspondences. The table is your shopping list and timing guide.

What are planetary hours and how do I calculate them?

Planetary hours divide daylight and darkness into 12 equal parts each, ruled by the 7 planets in Chaldean order (Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Sun, Venus, Mercury, Moon). Day hours = sunrise to sunset / 12. Night hours = sunset to sunrise / 12. Use apps (Astral Lab) or calculate manually: day length / 12 = hour length. Each hour ruled by planet in sequence starting with day ruler.

How do I cross-reference correspondence tables?

Cross-referencing protocol: 1) Identify primary axis (e.g., Mars). 2) Pull Mars row from planetary table. 3) Cross-reference: Mars color (red) → elemental table (Fire). Mars hour → temporal table (Tuesday, hour 1/8). Mars metal (iron) → mineral table. Mars herb (wormwood) → herbal table. Mars tarot (Tower) → tarot table. Build the resonant field across all tables.

How do I build my own correspondence tables?

1) Choose domain (planetary, elemental, etc.). 2) Define primary axis (rows). 3) Select 8+ correspondence columns. 4) Research authoritative sources (Agrippa, Crowley, Dee, Skinner, Tyson). 5) Format as APA table. 6) Cross-validate with 2+ sources. 7) Test in ritual: does the correspondence produce measurable effect? 8) Iterate and refine.
