The Definition: Egregore = Collective Thought-Form = Group Entity

An egregore is a collective thought-form — an autonomous entity sustained by the collective attention, belief, ritual, and emotional energy of a group. Unlike a servitor (single operator, specific task, personal off-switch), an egregore is:

  • Group-created: Born from collective intention, not individual will.
  • Collectively sustained: Fed by group attention, ritual, shared belief, emotional energy.
  • Potentially immortal: Can outlive founding members. Persists as long as feeding continues.
  • Governance-dependent: Requires group consensus for major decisions (including termination).
  • Autonomous potential: Can develop independent agency, influence members, resist dissolution.

The Egregore Equation: Egregore = (Collective Intention × Shared Ritual × Emotional Investment) / Time. The egregore is the group's externalized unconscious — it embodies the group's shadow, aspirations, fears, and aspirations.

Phase 4: Dissolution — Safe Termination & Schism Protocols

Every egregore must end. An egregore without an end is a parasite.

Standard Dissolution (Consensus)

  1. Consensus Vote: Governance threshold met for dissolution.
  2. Formal Revocation: "We, [GROUP], revoke [NAME]. Mandate complete. Return to source."
  3. Sigil Destruction: Burn physical. Digital: secure delete (7-pass) or encrypt + lose key.
  4. Energy Return: Visualize energy returning to each member's center. Do not keep.
  5. Banish: Group LBRP / Star Ruby / Gnostic Pentagram.
  6. Ground: Salt bath, water + salt, protein, 8h sleep.
  7. Journal: Date, final metrics, lessons, post-mortem.

Schism Protocol (Egregore Splits)

When governance fails and factions form:

  1. Detect: Two+ factions with irreconcilable mandate interpretations.
  2. Mediate: 7-day mediation window. External mediator if needed.
  3. Split or Dissolve: If mediation fails → formal schism. Each faction gets copy of spec, new sigil, new housing. Original egregore dissolved.
  4. Asset Division: Digital assets split per governance. Physical assets per consensus.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a servitor and an egregore?

Servitor = single operator, specific task, defined lifespan, personal off-switch. Egregore = group-created, collective purpose, potentially indefinite, requires group consensus to terminate. Servitors are tools; egregores are entities.

How do I create an egregore for my group?

1) Define spec (name, purpose, values, feeding, governance, dissolution). 2) Create sigil (Chaos Sigil Generator, group gnosis charge). 3) Build housing (physical/digital space). 4) Define feeding schedule + governance. 5. Birth: group gnosis + sigil charge + verbal declaration. 6. Feed per schedule. 7. Governance per spec.

Can an egregore become dangerous?

Yes. Unfed egregores become parasitic (drain members). Ungoverned egregores develop autonomy (act against members' interests). Schism = egregore splits into hostile factions. Prevention: strict feeding schedule, clear governance, defined off-switch, regular audits.

How do I dissolve an egregore?

1) Consensus decision (governance protocol). 2) Formal revocation ceremony. 3) Sigil destruction (burn physical, secure delete digital). 4. Group banishment (LBRP). 5. Grounding (salt, water, protein, sleep). 6. 72-hour quarantine. 7. Post-mortem audit.

What is an algorithmic egregore?

An algorithmic egregore is a collective thought-form sustained by networked human attention and algorithmic reinforcement. Social media feeds, recommendation algorithms, and viral content are egregores sustained by millions of human attention-hours, shaped by algorithmic selection pressure. They have autonomy, influence behavior, and can be invoked/banished like traditional egregores.
