Professional 7-Step Dressing Protocol (Complete)

  1. SELECT: Color + Timing. Use Candle Color Calculator. Match: Intent → Color → Planet → Day → Hour → Moon Phase.
  2. CLEANSE: Incense pass (frankincense, sage, palo santo). Visualize white light. Verbal: "I cleanse you for [exact intent]."
  3. CARVE: Sigil, runes, planetary glyph, target name, numbers. Direction: TOWARD you = attraction. AWAY = banishing. Depth: 2-3mm. Tools: pin, needle, ritual knife, scribing tool.
  4. ANOINT: Oil matched to color/planet. Attraction: base → tip (3 passes clockwise). Banishing: tip → base (3 passes counter-clockwise). Focus intent per pass.
  5. CHARGE: Hold candle. Gnosis (30-60 sec). Pour intent into wax. See flame burning. Verbal: "This candle IS [intent]."
  6. LIGHT: At exact planetary hour. Speak intent aloud. Read flame immediately (see Ceromancy below).
  7. MONITOR & CLOSE: Journal: time, flame behavior, sensations. Burn complete = ideal. Snuff = "Work continues." Dispose per intent (bury/crossroads/water).

Frequently Asked Questions

What candle color should I use for my intention?
White=purification/new beginnings, Green=money/growth, Red=passion/courage, Black=protection/banishing, Blue=healing/communication, Purple=spiritual power, Pink=love/self-love, Yellow=creativity/intellect, Orange=success/ambition, Brown=grounding/animals, Grey=neutrality/balance, Gold=solar power/divine connection, Silver=lunar power/intuition. Use the free Candle Color Calculator tool for exact matching.
Can I reuse a candle for multiple spells?
No. Each candle should be dedicated to ONE intention. Reusing mixes energies and dilutes focus. For small spells: birthday candles (10-15 min burn). For longer workings: glass-encased 7-day novenas or figure candles.
How long should I let a candle burn?
Ideally until it burns out completely (self-completing spell). If you must extinguish: use a snuffer or wet fingers — NEVER blow out (disperses energy). Relight at same planetary hour with same focused intent. Birthday candles: 15-30 min. Taper candles: 1-3 hours. 7-day novenas: continuous or daily relighting.
Do I need to be Wiccan or follow a specific tradition?
Absolutely not. Candle magic is non-denominational and appears in Hoodoo, Chaos Magick, folk magic, Santería, Catholic folk practice, and countless traditions. All you need is intention, focus, and the willingness to do the work. No initiation required.
What if the candle flame behaves strangely?
Flame reading is part of the practice: Steady/bright = clear path, Flickering = resistance/outside interference, Dancing high = strong energy/spirit presence, Low/smoky = blockage/need more charge, Blue tint = spirit presence, Crackling/popping = message incoming, Double flame = dual forces/partnership. Document in journal.
How do I dispose of candle remains?
Attraction spells: bury wax on your property or in potted plant (growth). Banishing spells: discard at crossroads, in running water, or trash far from home. Protection: bury at property boundaries. Love: bury near front door. Money: bury near wallet/safe or keep wax in money jar.
Can I do candle magic for someone else?
Yes, with their informed consent. Carve their name, use their hair/nail clippings/photo as link. Ethical guideline: never bind, control, or harm. Focus on: healing, clarity, opportunity, protection. Document consent in journal.
What's the difference between taper, votive, and 7-day candles?
Taper (12-18"): 2-4 hour burns, precise carving, elegant. Votive (2"): 10-15 hour, container needed, good for multi-day. 7-day glass novena: 100+ hours, continuous or daily relight, best for long workings. Figure candles: human/animal shapes for specific targets. Birthday candles: 10-15 min, quick spells.
How do I know if my spell worked?
Track in journal: Date, intent, color, moon phase, planetary hour, flame behavior, dreams/synchronicities in 72 hours, tangible results in 28 days (lunar cycle). Success markers: opportunity appears, obstacle removes, feeling shifts, dream confirmation, third-party confirmation. Review at 7, 28, 90 days.
