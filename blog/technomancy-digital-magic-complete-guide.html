Frequently Asked Questions

What is technomancy and how does it differ from traditional magic?

Technomancy is the integration of digital technology with magical practice. It recognizes that technology itself is a magical system: algorithms as sigils, networks as egregores, code as spells, the screen as scrying mirror. It's not using tech to supplement magic; it's recognizing that digital systems ARE magical systems operating on the same principles of pattern, intention, and resonance.

Can code really be a spell?

Yes. Code is intention encoded in logic. When executed, it enacts change in the world. The programmer's intention is encoded in the logic. The execution enacts the will. This is the definition of a spell: intention encoded in a medium, executed to produce change. The medium changed from herbs/words to logic/electricity; the principle is identical.

What is an algorithmic egregore?

Enter gnosis via extended exhale + soft gaze. The screen's refresh rate (60-144Hz) provides a subtle flicker that can entrain brainwaves.

What are digital sigils and how do they differ from traditional?
