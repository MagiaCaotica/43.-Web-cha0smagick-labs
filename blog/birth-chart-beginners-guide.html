Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Sun, Moon, and Rising signs?
Sun sign = your core identity, ego, and life drive. Moon sign = your emotional nature, instincts, and what you need to feel safe. Rising sign (Ascendant) = your outer personality, physical appearance, first impressions, and how you approach new situations. All three form your 'Big Three' — the foundation of chart reading.
How many houses are in a birth chart and what do they mean?
12 houses, each representing a life area: 1st=Self/Appearance, 2nd=Money/Values, 3rd=Communication, 4th=Home/Family, 5th=Creativity/Romance, 6th=Health/Work, 7th=Relationships, 8th=Transformation/Shared Resources, 9th=Travel/Philosophy, 10th=Career/Public Image, 11th=Community/Friends, 12th=Spirituality/Unconscious.
Do I need my exact birth time for an accurate chart?
Yes, for Rising sign and house cusps. The Ascendant changes every ~2 hours. Without exact time: Sun/Moon/planet signs are still accurate (mostly), but Rising sign, house cusps, and house placements will be approximate. Use 12:00 PM as default but mark as 'time unknown.' Astral Lab app handles unknown time gracefully.
What are the major aspects and what do they mean?
Conjunction (0°) = fusion/intensity. Opposition (180°) = polarity/awareness through other. Square (90°) = tension/growth catalyst. Trine (120°) = natural talent/flow. Sextile (60°) = opportunity/ease. Hard aspects (square, opposition) drive evolution. Soft aspects (trine, sextile) show gifts.
Can I read charts for other people?
Yes, with their birth data. Ethical guideline: only read with consent. Focus on empowerment, not prediction. Never diagnose medical/psychological conditions. Synastry (relationship charts) requires both parties' consent. Astral Lab includes synastry mode.
What if I don't know my birth time?
Use 12:00 PM as placeholder. Sun, Moon, and planet signs will be ~95% accurate. Rising sign and house cusps will be wrong. You can still work with: planet signs, aspects between planets, Moon phase, and solar house system (Sun = 1st house cusp). Astral Lab has 'Unknown Time' mode.
How often should I check transits?
Daily: Moon transits (mood/timing). Weekly: Mercury/Venus/Mars (communication/love/action). Monthly: Sun (focus), Jupiter (expansion windows). Quarterly: Saturn (structure lessons), outer planets (generational shifts). Astral Lab sends transit alerts for your natal placements.
What is a chart ruler and why does it matter?
The chart ruler is the planet that rules your Rising sign. Example: Rising in Scorpio → Mars (traditional) / Pluto (modern) is chart ruler. Its sign/house/aspects show HOW you navigate life and WHERE your life force directs. It's the 'engine' of your chart.
