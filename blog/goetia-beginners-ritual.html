What Is Goetia and Why Beginners Try It

Goetia is the branch of Solomonic magic that deals with the 72 spirits catalogued in the Ars Goetia, the first book of the Lesser Key of Solomon. Beginners are drawn to it for practical reasons: spirits are traditionally associated with specific outcomes — wealth (Asmoday), knowledge (Dantalion), influence (Bael), love and reconciliation (Sitri).

The single most common question in r/goetia and r/occult is "how do I safely invoke a goetic spirit for beginners?" — and most answers are either academic (read Mathers, read Crowley) or intimidating (buy a sword, a circle, an incense burner, three days of purification). This guide gives you the minimal safe method that actually works, with no hardware required.

The 72 Spirits of the Ars Goetia Explained

The 72 spirits are organized in a hierarchy of kings, dukes, princes, marquises, counts, knights and presidents, each with a seal, a name, a rank and a set of abilities. You do not need to memorize all 72 before your first working — you need the correct seal of the spirit you intend to contact and nothing else.

Where beginners fail: drawing the seal wrong. Seals are sacred geometry — a misplaced stroke changes the binding. The safest approach is to use an app that renders the seal for you in the correct proportions (more on this below) or to print it from a verified source. Hand-drawing seals is a legitimate skill, but it is not the place to start.

Safety Before You Start: The 5 Golden Rules

  • 1. Never skip the protective circle. It is not decoration; it defines the space of authority you operate from.
  • 2. State the intent out loud before you begin. Vague intentions invite vague results.
  • 3. Have a banishing ritual ready. The LBRP (Lesser Banishing Ritual of the Pentagram) takes 5 minutes and is the standard reset.
  • 4. Never invoke for harm or coercion. The ethics discussion is real; work with the spirit's own domain instead.
  • 5. Journal everything. Date, time, seal, enn, request, result. If it does not work, the journal is what lets you debug it.

The Beginner Ritual: Step by Step

This method works for a first evocation and takes about 30 minutes. Do it at night, alone, with your phone in airplane mode.

  1. Prepare the space. Clean the room, open a window, turn off notifications. Dim light or candlelight only.
  2. Draw or display the protective circle. A physical circle (string, chalk, tape) or a digital rendering of the circle of art both work.
  3. Place the seal. The seal of the spirit goes inside the circle with you, or directly in front of you on paper.
  4. Enter a light gnosis state. 5-10 minutes of steady breathing until your mind is quiet — no visualization required yet.
  5. Chant the enn. Each spirit has an enn, a vocal key. Repeat it slowly 9 times while focusing on the seal. (See the section below.)
  6. Make your request. In plain, specific language: "I ask [spirit] to assist with [exact outcome]." Do not negotiate; request once, clearly.
  7. Close. Thank the spirit, dismiss it by name, perform a banishing, extinguish the candle and write the journal entry.

Enn Chants and Seals

The enn is a chanted vocalization that attunes the practitioner to the spirit's frequency. A well-known example is Asmoday's: "Ayer secore on ca Asmoday". Enns are not in the original grimoire — they come from the modern Solomonic revival — but the community standard is overwhelming.

5 Common Beginner Mistakes

  • 1. Asking for something vague. "Help me with money" fails; "assist me in obtaining a signed contract worth $4,000 before September 30" has a target.
  • 2. Repeating the ritual daily out of anxiety. Once is a working; twice is desperation; a third time cancels the first.
  • 3. Skipping the banishing "because nothing appeared." The banishing closes the circuit regardless of visible results.
  • 4. Using a photocopied seal from a blog. Verify proportions or render digitally.
  • 5. Telling everyone about it. Skeptic energy is real; keep the working quiet until it manifests.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is goetia dangerous for beginners?

Not inherently. The risk is almost entirely in sloppy protocol: skipped circle, vague intent, no banishing. Follow the five golden rules above and the ritual is safer than most people assume.

Do I need to know all 72 demons before starting?

No. You need the correct seal and enn of the one spirit you are contacting, plus the protective protocol. Memorization is for later.

Can I do goetia without the circle of art?

Technically yes, but you should not. The circle is your authority boundary. Start with it; experiment later.

What if the spirit does not appear?

Journal the attempt, check the seal accuracy and your gnosis depth, then wait 72 hours before retrying. Most first-attempt "failures" are seal or intent errors.
