TECELÓN

By Frater Alek0s ⬢ September 27, 2026 ⬢ 8 min read

Money workings in this tradition come with a vocabulary, and the vocabulary is where most of the difficulty lives. Correspondence tables, numerological reductions, planetary dignities, sigil methods, each with its own internal logic and its own capacity to waste your afternoon. The useful move is to build the minimal set you actually need and know what each piece is for, because a table you do not understand produces a reading you cannot check. This reference piece works through the terms you need, the tables worth building and the ones worth ignoring, the imperative-mood rule that does more work than any correspondence, and how to reconcile a working against a ledger without either flattering yourself or abandoning the method before it has had a fair run.

The working vocabulary, defined once

A sigil is a glyph reduced from a statement of intent. Sigilisation is the reduction process. A charge is the short sequence that attaches the glyph to a state. An office is what the working is for, in one imperative sentence. A term is how long you will run it before judging. A ledger is the record of what you actually did, which is the only record that makes the other five meaningful.

That is the whole vocabulary needed for a money working, and the order matters. Office first, because a working with no office has no success criterion. Sigilisation second, because the reduction is easy and should not absorb the thinking. Charge third, briefly. Term and ledger fourth, because they are the parts that get skipped and they are the parts that produce the result.

Correspondence, and the job it is hired for

Correspondence is the doctrine that things share qualities and can act on each other through those shared qualities. It is the organising principle of traditional magic and it is also the part most likely to generate confident nonsense if used without care, because any two objects can be described as sharing a quality if you are willing to stretch the description.

The job it is actually hired for in a money working is narrow: it names your aim in a vocabulary with some history behind it, which makes the aim more specific. A sigil for improving the harvest is vaguer than a sigil for improving the rent from this specific building, and correspondence is one tool for that narrowing. Beyond that use, tables produce readings you cannot verify, and unverifiable readings in a money context are expensive because they substitute for action.

The borrowed twelve, and how to use it honestly

The number 741 appears in abundance material because its digits sum to twelve, a figure that recurs across numerological and astrological schemes. The practice borrows the association and does not need a physical mechanism to justify it, which is the honest position and also a modest one.

A short numeric string is a good focusing device because it is easier to hold in mind than a full sentence, and that is the entire reason sigilisation exists. If the number works for you, use it as a handle for attention rather than as a description of how money arrives. Anyone who tells you the digits attract currency is selling you something, and the price is usually a course.

The imperative, written before the glyph

The single most consequential formatting decision is grammatical mood. Requests leave room for negotiation with yourself, and the negotiation is where the effort disappears. Commands do not. I will send five applications before the tenth. I will raise my day rate by twenty percent on the next quote. I will invoice on the same day work is delivered.

Write the imperative before the reduction and never go back to fix the sentence afterwards. The reduction is mechanical and the thinking is not, and people who draft in glyph form end up with a shape that encodes vagueness. If you cannot write a sentence with a verb and a date in it, the aiming is not finished, no matter how good the sigil looks.

A first abundance working, end to end

Forty minutes, one sitting. Fifteen minutes drafting the office with a number and a deadline. Five reducing to a glyph. Five charging in a fixed sequence, with the target state being the felt sense rather than the figure. Fifteen writing the if-then plan, which is the operational half of the working and the half most people never write.

Then a fortnight of ordinary execution, with a one-line-per-day ledger. The charge is the shortest part of this and the fortnight is the working. If the two are being ranked, the ranking is wrong, and the practitioners who make that error are the ones whose money workings produce a satisfying charge and no money.

Two tables: the one you build and the one you avoid

Build one table and it should be small: a list of numerals associated with outcomes you can actually observe. Rent received. Invoices settled. Applications out. That is a table in the only sense that matters, which is that it lets you check a claim against a number.

Avoid planetary dignity tables, complete numerological correspondences, and anything requiring you to calculate a chart to determine whether today is a good day to send an email. These are not forbidden, they are a way of deferring the action indefinitely, and the deferral feels like diligence. A correspondence reading is worth the time you spend on it. If it takes twenty minutes to find a favourable date for a two-minute email, it has become a substitute for the email.

Where a correspondence reading misleads

The specific failure is the unfalsifiable match. A table assigns a quality to a number, an object, a planet, and a day, and any event can be described in terms that connect to it after the fact. Read in reverse, after a good month, correspondence will explain the month. Read in advance, before the work, it will explain the intention.

The safeguard is direction of reading. Use correspondence forward, to make the aim more specific, and never backward, to explain an outcome. If you find yourself able to account for last month's results in symbolic terms, the accounting has nothing to do with causation, and treating it as a confirmation is how practitioners convince themselves that a method works when the evidence is only the story of why it should.

Notebook, ledger, and the two hours

Two artefacts, both in the same notebook if you like. The working note, with the office, the sigil, the date, and the term. The ledger, one line per day for the fortnight, with the date, the action, and a result if there is one.

The total time investment is about two hours across a fortnight, and the ledger takes thirty seconds a day. That ratio is the point. Money practice with a high ceremony cost and a low ledger cost reliably produces a satisfying charge and no change in income, because the time went into preparation and none went into the actions the sigil was aiming at.

Reconciling the working against the ledger

At the end of the term, put the two documents side by side. The ledger tells you which step failed, and the diagnosis is specific. Ten applications and no replies is targeting. Ten conversations and no second call is pitch. No applications at all is commitment, and no sigil fixes commitment. One quote sent and one accepted is a small real result and worth more as information than as money.

Most money workings do not fail at the charging stage. They fail at the ledger stage, in the sense that the actions were never taken, and the diagnosis is available in advance if you keep the ledger. The record exists to make the failure legible, which is the only way to correct it rather than to abandon the method and start a new one next month.

What the numbers will not tell you

A single month is noise. Income is dominated by contract cycles, payment terms, and factors outside your control, and a working that coincides with a good month has told you almost nothing. This is the reason for a term rather than an open-ended practice: an arbitrary stopping point is what stops you from reading every good month as confirmation.

The numbers also cannot distinguish luck from method, and with a sample size of one month they never will. What they can do is confirm the process, which is the part you control. Actions executed at the specified rate is a real and checkable result, and it is the one worth chasing, because process is what repeats and money is what follows.

What an abundance practice owes the people around it

The line is between influencing your own decisions and influencing other people's without their knowledge. A sigil aimed at your application rate, your pricing, or your published output is aimed at you. A sigil aimed at a named client or a particular employer is an attempt to act on a decision that is not yours, and the outcome is not yours to interpret.

There is also the question of what happens when it works. Sudden income arrives with obligations attached, and someone who has spent a month in scarcity can commit to things a more settled person would decline. If a working produces what you asked for, the useful follow-up is a second working about the obligations, which is duller to write and more useful to have written.

Rebuilding a working that stopped paying

Refresh by rewriting, not by re-charging. A sigil that has run a month has authorised a month of decisions, and those decisions are over. Take the original office, update the figure or the specific action, and reduce a new glyph. Same aim, current form, fresh term.

If two consecutive terms produce a clean ledger and no change in outcome, the method is not the problem and the aim is. Go back to the drafting step and write a different sentence, usually one with a smaller number or a nearer deadline. The pattern across a year of workings is usually that the earlier aims were too large to execute, and the ledger has been telling you that the whole time.

Frequently Asked Questions

What tools do I actually need for a money sigil?

A pen, paper, and a sigil generator if you want one. Everything beyond that is optional, and the optional things are where the time goes. A spreadsheet with one row per day is worth having because the diagnosis of a failed term depends entirely on the process record existing. Correspondences tables are optional and frequently a way of not sending the email.

How do I know if my correspondence is real or invented?

Check the direction of reading. Correspondence used forward, to make an aim more specific, is doing legitimate work. Used backward, to account for an outcome that already happened, it will explain anything, because any event can be described in terms that connect to it. The test is whether you used it before or after the results were in.

Should I use planetary or lunar timing for a money working?

As a marker, yes. A chosen date puts a boundary on the commitment and makes the start easier to begin. As a cause of financial change, there is no support, and the calculation is where a working quietly stalls. Pick a date, write it down, and treat the transit chart as decoration you were going to enjoy anyway.

My first term had a clean ledger and no change in income. What now?

Read the ledger for which step stalled. Actions executed with no replies is a targeting or pitch problem and needs a new office, not a new sigil method. No actions at all is a commitment problem, and the fix is a smaller number and a nearer deadline. A clean ledger is a good result even when the money did not move, because it tells you where to aim.

Is an abundance working manipulative if it involves other people?

Only when the target is another person's decision. A sigil for finding work is aimed at your own application rate and involves no one else. A sigil naming a specific client is an attempt to influence a choice that person has not agreed to, and the result is not yours to count. Keep the named party out of the office and the question does not arise.
