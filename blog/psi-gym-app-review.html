What Is PSI GYM?

PSI GYM is a focused ESP training app built around the classic Zener card protocol. Instead of a generic "brain training" app, it gives you a statistically honest psi testing environment: you run sessions, log your hits, and watch your deviation from chance over time. It is a one-time $3.99 purchase with no subscription and no data collection, which is rare in this category.

How the Zener Card Test Works

The Zener deck has five symbols: circle, cross, wavy lines, square, and star. In a standard session the app shows you one symbol at a time and you guess the next one. Pure chance is exactly 20% per card, so anything meaningfully above that across hundreds of trials starts to look like real signal.

  • Classic 25-card run (5 of each symbol)
  • Session history with hit-rate per symbol
  • Deviation tracking so you can see streaks that are not luck
  • Custom run lengths for longer focused practice

Daily Intuition Training

Psi ability behaves like a muscle: it needs daily, low-volume reps. The app supports short daily sessions (2–5 minutes) that fit into a morning routine, plus a journal so you can correlate your psi scores with sleep, mood, and magical practice days.

The Science Behind ESP Training

The Zener protocol was introduced by J.B. Rhine at Duke University in the 1930s and remains the reference standard for controlled psi testing. Modern ganzfeld studies continue to report hit rates above chance, and many practitioners treat structured feedback as the fastest path to improving intuitive accuracy. PSI GYM gives you that feedback loop without the noise.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a magickal servitor?

A magickal servitor is a programmed thought-form that executes specific tasks on the astral plane. It is a core tool in chaos magick, systematized by Spare and Carroll.

How do I create a magickal servitor?

Follow the four-phase framework: Design (purpose, sigil, traits, anchor) ? Activation (gnosis, charging, naming, launching) ? Maintenance (feeding, refreshing) ? Dismissal (expiration, destruction, cleansing). See our step-by-step servitor creation guide for detailed instructions.

What is the difference between a servitor and an egregore?

Servitors are individual creations with limited purpose and lifespan. Egregores are collective thought-forms maintained by groups, capable of evolving beyond their original programming. Learn more in our egregore creation guide.

Can a servitor become dangerous?

Yes, if poorly designed or neglected. Always set a programmed lifespan, maintain regular feeding, and pre-plan a dismissal ritual. The Magical Servitors Manual provides complete safety protocols.
