Frequently Asked Questions

What is a flaneur?

A flaneur is a person who walks through cities without fixed purpose, observing and absorbing the environment. The term originated in 19th-century Paris and has been adopted by psychogeographers and chaos magicians as a practice of receptive awareness.

How is purposeless wandering a magical practice?

Without a fixed destination, the environment itself becomes the oracle. Every turn, every encounter, every observation is a potential message. The practitioner enters a state of active receptivity that is itself a form of gnosis.
