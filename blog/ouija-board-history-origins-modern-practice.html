Phase 1: Pre-History — Precursors & Precursors (1500s-1880s)

The ouija board did not appear from nothing. It is the mechanical automation of centuries of spirit communication practice.

Automatic Writing & The Planchette Precursors (16th-18th Century)

  • John Dee & Edward Kelley (1580s): Enochian magic via "scrying stone" and automatic writing. The first systematic spirit communication protocol.
  • Planchette Precursors (1600s-1700s): "Writing planchettes" — heart-shaped wooden tools with pencil attached. Used for automatic writing in French salons and esoteric circles. The planchette = mechanical automation of automatic writing.
  • Mesmerism & Animal Magnetism (1770s-1840s): Franz Mesmer → animal magnetism → trance states → automatic writing as "magnetic sleep" phenomenon.

The Spiritualist Explosion (1848-1880s)

  • The Fox Sisters (1848): Hydesville, NY rappings. "Mr. Splitfoot" communication via knocks. Birth of Modern Spiritualism.
  • Table Turning & Tipping (1850s): Tables as spirit communication devices. Tilting, rotating, levitating. Precursor to planchette movement.
  • Automatic Writing Craze (1850s-1880s): Mediums producing reams of spirit dictation. The planchette = mechanical automation of this practice.
  • Scientific Investigation: Michael Faraday (1853) investigates table turning → discovers ideomotor effect. The scientific explanation precedes the commercial board by decades.

Key Insight:

The ouija board did not CREATE spirit communication. It INDUSTRIALIZED it. The planchette automated what mediums did by hand. The board standardized the circuit.

Phase 2: Commercial Birth — The Planchette & The Ouija Brand (1868-1920)

The Writing Planchette (1868-1886)

  • 1868: G.W. Cottrell (Boston) patents first commercial planchette. Heart-shaped, pencil-attached. Sold as "writing planchette" for automatic writing.
  • 1868-1886: Planchette craze in US/Europe. Sold as parlor amusement AND serious spirit tool. 100,000+ sold in first year.
  • Limitation: Pencil wears down, paper tears, handwriting illegible. The board solves this.

The Ouija Board Patent (1890-1891)

  • 1890: Elijah Bond & Charles Kennard file patent for "Ouija or Egyptian luck board." Board + planchette WITHOUT pencil. Letters printed ON BOARD.
  • 1891: William Fuld joins. "Ouija" trademarked (French 'oui' + German 'ja' = "yes-yes"). Kennard Novelty Company formed.
  • 1892: William Fuld takes control. Renames to Ouija Novelty Company. Aggressive marketing begins.

The William Fuld Empire (1892-1927)

  • Marketing Genius: "Ouija" = exotic (French 'oui' + German 'ja' = "yes-yes"). Mystery branding.
  • Family Game Positioning: "Mysterious oracle for family entertainment." Removes occult stigma for mass market.
  • Manufacturing Scale: 1920s: 2+ million boards sold. Fuld factories in Baltimore. "Ouija" becomes genericized trademark.

The Parker Brothers Acquisition (1966)

  • Parker Brothers buys William Fuld Mfg. Rebrands as family board game. "Ouija: The Mystifying Oracle."
  • Occult community CONTINUES serious use. The "toy" framing never stopped occult practitioners.

Frequently Asked Questions

When was the ouija board invented?

The planchette (writing tool) dates to 1868. The 'Ouija' branded board was patented by Elijah Bond and Charles Kennard in 1890, trademarked as 'Ouija' in 1891 by William Fuld. The planchette as writing tool predates the board by ~20 years.

Was the ouija board always a toy/game?

No. Originally marketed as a 'talking board' for spirit communication during the Spiritualist craze. Parker Brothers acquired it in 1966 and rebranded as a family game. The occult community never stopped using it as a serious divination tool.

How does the ouija board actually work?

The ideomotor effect: unconscious muscular movements reflect subconscious expectations. For chaos magick, this is a FEATURE not a bug — the board provides direct access to the subconscious mind. The mechanism IS the magic.

How did the ouija board become associated with evil/demons?

Hollywood (The Exorcist 1973, Witchboard 1986) cemented the demonic association. The Spiritualist movement was already controversial. Religious opposition + horror films = cultural meme. The mechanism (ideomotor) is neutral; the cultural framing is the variable.
