Yes-No Tarot Spread Guide

A yes-no reading is one card, one question, one clear answer. The key is phrasing: questions must be answerable by yes or no and asked in the present tense. Cups and Wands lean yes; Swords and Pentacles lean no; Major Arcana confirm the importance of the outcome; reversals flip the answer. For clarity, pull a second card as advice after the yes-no answer. This turns a binary read into an actionable next step.
