SKRUDRANOZCON

By Frater Alek0s ⬢ September 27, 2026 ⬢ 8 min read

SKRUDRANOZCON is a name that behaves differently from most names in the Goetia, and the difference matters before any ritual is attempted. A great many of the seventy-two names have a traceable trail through the Conjuratio Ben-Maimon, the Lemuria and the Pseudepigrapha, and a stated rank, form, office and set of powers. Others arrive with a thin entry and almost no independent attestation. SKRUDRANOZCON sits in the second category, which means the work here is not really about contacting something out there. It is about what you are doing when you take an unverified name and give it a defined job, and about how to tell in advance whether that job is one you should be delegating at all.

A name with very little behind it

The first honest step is to establish how much is actually documented. Many Goetic names come with a long pedigree through medieval and early-modern demonological compilation, and you can trace them. When a name appears with a single modern appearance, no clear office and no consistent description across sources, the honest description is that you do not know what it is.

That is not a defect in the working. It is the condition of the working. A name with no fixed meaning can be assigned one, and the assignment is entirely yours, which means the discipline is not in discovering what SKRUDRANOZCON wants but in deciding, in advance and in writing, what you are asking it to do. If you cannot state that in one sentence, you are not ready to charge anything.

Evocation, invocation, and the difference that keeps getting collapsed

Classical ceremonial usage distinguishes two acts. Evocation means calling a spirit to appear, so that something is present to be seen and addressed. Invocation means inviting a spirit's influence to operate through the operator, so that the working happens in the practitioner's own act rather than in a visible presence. Most modern usage erases the distinction and uses both words for the same thing.

Which one you are doing changes what evidence you should expect. Evocation is vulnerable to the question of whether anything appeared. Invocation has no visible event to inspect, and its only honest sign is whether the intended effect occurred. Choose deliberately, and if you invoke, write down the intended effect before the working rather than discovering afterwards whether you would count it.

What the Goetia catalogue hands you for free

For the better-documented spirits, the Ars Goetia supplies a rank, a visible form, an office and a list of powers. That amount of structure is why the catalogue has been used continuously for centuries: it removes almost all of the design work and leaves only the execution. The structure also constrains you, because an office implies limits, and a spirit described as a keeper of records is not thereby a general-purpose agent.

When a name is thin on exactly these fields, the structure has to be supplied by you instead. Write the missing fields yourself, in a fixed format, and mark which parts are drawn from the source text and which parts you invented. This is not scepticism for its own sake. It is what stops an open assignment from quietly expanding to cover every task you would rather not do yourself.

Drafting a working from a bare name

A usable draft has five parts: the name as you will write it, the office in one sentence, the specific powers you are authorising, the specific powers you are explicitly withholding, and the conditions under which you consider the work finished. The withholding clause is the part beginners skip and the part that does the most work afterwards.

Keep it to one page. A long brief produces a long charge, a long charge produces a diffuse result, and a diffuse result cannot be evaluated. If the office sentence contains the word and more than twice, the scope has drifted and you should rewrite it before proceeding rather than after the first disappointing week.

The charge, step by step

Begin by writing the office aloud once, then destroying the written copy. The gesture matters less than the transition it enforces: the brief has been transferred from a document you might revise into a commitment you can no longer edit mid-working. Then the sigil, cut or drawn in one continuous line, and then the statement of intent read over it in the same words you wrote.

End by dismissing. Classical sources treat the closing formula as a formal requirement rather than a courtesy, and functionally it does something you will come to rely on: it establishes that the working has a shape, and therefore that it can end. Leaving a working open is the most common cause of the vague, low-grade sense of unfinished business that beginners report weeks later.

Narrowing the office before you charge it

The most reliable single improvement to this kind of working is subtraction. A request for wealth, a request for clarity and a request for the return of a former partner are three different offices, and a spirit assigned all three has no defined behaviour to display. Pick one, state the boundary of it, and write down what you will treat as failure.

Where the office involves anyone who is not you, the boundary is not optional. Whether the working is a self-assignment or a named target changes its ethical status completely, and the case for a narrow office is strongest precisely where the target is another person, because a broad office aimed at a person tends to produce consequences you did not authorise and cannot undo.

Telling enthusiasm from a result

The reliable control is a paired run. Charge the working once, then perform an identical charge for a deliberately trivial and easily checked outcome, and compare your reaction to the two. People who have just done ritual are alert, oriented and looking for meaning, and that state alone produces vivid subjective impressions in both cases.

Keep a running log of results you predicted, dated before the fact, and review it monthly with no other input. If the log is empty for two months, the honest conclusion is that you do not yet have evidence, which is a legitimate place to be and a much better foundation than a stack of vivid recollections that cannot be checked against anything.

Paper, ink, and the hours worth spending

A sheet of paper, a pen, and a sigil drawn in one line is the entire toolkit, and the simplicity is not asceticism. Every additional element adds a variable you will not be able to isolate later, so a working that fails becomes impossible to diagnose. An alphabet cipher, if you use one, is worth the small effort because it makes the name reproducible without re-drawing it each time.

Timing matters less than practitioners suggest, but consistency does. Charge at the same point in the day and the same day of the week if you can manage it, because a scheduled working gives you a natural checkpoint to stop at, and an unscheduled one runs until you are tired, which is exactly the wrong state for a commit operation.

Keeping the charge legible after week two

The file is the working. One page holding the office, the sigil, the date, the predicted sign and the actual result, and nothing else. Add to it rather than rewriting it, so that the sequence of predictions stays intact and your own revisions of the aim remain visible rather than being tidied away.

Revisit on a fixed schedule, monthly for three months, then quarterly. If after a year the file contains one completed charge and no predicted sign, the honest move is to treat the practice as unconfirmed and either restate the office more precisely or set it down. Re-charging the same ambiguous working is not persistence, it is repetition without feedback.

Which signs to log and which to ignore

Log external events you could not have predicted, and log them the day they happen. Everything internal belongs in a different column, marked as internal, because internal experience is exactly what a charged state of mind will produce whether or not anything external occurs. Dreams, coincidences, remembered conversations and mood shifts all belong to that second column.

Be especially sceptical of the sign that arrives during the charge itself. Feelings of presence, of pressure, of heat or of a reply are common responses to sustained concentration in a quiet room. They are worth noting and they are worth nothing as evidence, and the discipline of keeping the two columns separate is the whole of the difference between practice and self-confirmation.

Where the consent line sits

A working that affects only you requires that you stay the author. The moment the office reaches another person's preferences, sensations or decisions, you are working on someone who did not commission the work, and the fact that your tradition may hold your will to be the operative ingredient does not settle the question, it sharpens it. The classical framework expected a negotiation with a separate intelligence, and there is no negotiation available when the only active ingredient is you.

The practical rule that survives both the ceremonial and the sceptical reading is simple: aim workings at your own perception, effort and circumstances, and address other people through direct conversation. There is no version of this work that is made acceptable by adding ritual, and several that are made considerably worse by it.

Closing a working you are abandoning

If you decide to stop, say so explicitly and in writing, retire the sigil from the page rather than tearing it, and state in the file that the office is closed with no result recorded. An ambiguous close leaves the file in a state where every later entry can be read as a delayed answer, and that ambiguity will pull you back into re-charging at exactly the moments you have decided to stop.

Then do not reopen it for a cooling period of at least three months, and reopen it only with a rewritten office. Most of the discomfort of abandoning a working comes from having left the thread open, not from the decision itself. A closed file is a decision you can live with; an open one is an obligation you did not consent to carry.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SKRUDRANOZCON a real spirit?

There is no good evidence that any named spirit exists outside the human mind that works with it, and for a thinly attested name like this one there is very little historical material either. That is not an obstacle to a working, provided you are honest about what the exercise is. You are assigning a defined job to a name you have chosen, and the discipline lies in the precision of the job, not in the ontological status of the name.

Should I use a Goetia app or table to design the sigil?

A catalogue is useful for the documented entries because it saves you from inventing the office, and it is misleading for the thin ones because the invented entries are padded with confident detail that has no source. Whichever you use, write the office sentence yourself in your own words, and check the sigil by hand rather than accepting a generated glyph you have not traced yourself.

How long before I should expect anything?

There is no defensible number, and any source giving you one is describing an intention rather than a schedule. What you can do is set a review date in advance, write down the effect you expect and by when, and hold to that date. If nothing has happened, you have learned something about the reliability of the method, which is a better outcome than an indefinite wait that ends in a reinterpretation.

Can I work with a spirit to improve my finances?

Set the office to something about your own behaviour rather than about an amount. Requests aimed at a target person are where the ethical difficulty lives, and requests aimed at an outcome tend to produce an unfalsifiable result that you will interpret generously forever. A narrow office about your own earning effort gives you something you can actually check at the end of the month.

What if I feel a strong presence during the working?

Note it, log it in the internal column, and do not treat it as confirmation. A feeling of presence is a common product of sustained concentration in a quiet room and it appears in control runs too. What would count is an external event you could not have predicted, dated and written down beforehand. Without that, the feeling is interesting and it is not evidence.
