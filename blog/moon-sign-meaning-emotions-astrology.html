Why the Moon Sign Matters More Than Your Sun Sign in Relationships

Under stress, everyone regresses to their Moon sign behavior. A Fire Moon needs space to vent and act; an Earth Moon needs physical grounding and routine; an Air Moon needs to talk it out logically; a Water Moon needs emotional validation and closeness.
