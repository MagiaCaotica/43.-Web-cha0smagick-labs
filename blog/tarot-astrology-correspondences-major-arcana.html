Tarot and Astrology Correspondences: Mapping Major Arcana to Planets and Signs

By Frater Alek0s • July 21, 2026 • Updated September 15, 2026 • 25 min read

Two Systems, One Language

The correspondences between tarot and astrology are one of the richest areas of esoteric study. Each Major Arcana card corresponds to a planet, zodiac sign, Hebrew letter, and a path on the Tree of Life. The Magician corresponds to Mercury and Beth. The Empress corresponds to Venus and Daleth. The Wheel of Fortune corresponds to Jupiter and Kaph. These correspondences add exponential depth to readings — a card carries not only its tarot meaning but also its astrological timing, elemental quality, and qabalistic pathway.

Thoth / Crowley-Harris Variant Correspondences

Aleister Crowley's Thoth Tarot (with Lady Frieda Harris) modifies two key attributions:

  • The Emperor (IV) → Aries (same as Golden Dawn)
  • The Star (XVII) → Aquarius (same as Golden Dawn)

Wait — the swap is actually: Golden Dawn has The Emperor=Aries (IV) and The Star=Aquarius (XVII). Crowley keeps these the same. The confusion arises because some later authors (e.g., Robert Wang) misattribute. The actual Thoth shifts are:

  • Justice (XI) ↔ Strength (VIII) — Crowley swapped the numbers: Justice becomes VIII, Strength becomes XI, to align with Leo=Strength (VIII) and Libra=Justice (XI) in zodiacal order.
  • The Star (XVII) remains Aquarius in both systems.
Practical Rule: Pick ONE system (Golden Dawn or Thoth) and use it consistently. Mixing creates confusion. Golden Dawn is standard for Waite-Smith decks; Thoth is standard for Thoth decks.

Using Correspondences in Readings: Practical Protocols

Protocol 1: Natal Chart Overlay Method

Step-by-Step: Mapping Cards to the Querent's Natal Chart

Step 1: Obtain the querent's natal chart (date, time, location). Use Natal Chart tool for instant calculation.
Step 2: Identify the querent's dominant planets — planets on angles (ASC, DSC, MC, IC), in their own signs, or making the most aspects.
Step 3: When a Major Arcana appears, check its planetary ruler. If that planet is dominant in the natal chart, the card's message has double force — it speaks to a core life theme.
Step 4: Check the corresponding astrological house (see table above). The house shows the life area where the card's energy manifests most concretely.
Step 5: Note the Hebrew letter and Tree of Life path. If the querent has natal placements in the corresponding sephiroth (via planetary rulership), the card activates that specific pathway.

Protocol 3: Elemental Dignities in Spreads

When multiple Major Arcana appear together, their elemental natures modify each other:

  • Fire + Fire (Wands) — Intensifies: rapid action, inspiration, potential burnout.
  • Water + Water (Cups) — Deepens: emotional flooding, psychic amplification, possible overwhelm.
  • Air + Air (Swords) — Clarifies or over-intellectualizes: mental clarity vs. analysis paralysis.
  • Earth + Earth (Pentacles) — Stabilizes or stagnates: material manifestation vs. rigidity.
  • Fire + Water — Steam: transformation through emotional alchemy.
  • Fire + Air — Lightning: sudden insight, mental breakthroughs.
  • Fire + Earth — Lava: creative manifestation, slow but powerful.
  • Water + Air — Mist: intuitive downloads, dreams, unclear communication.
  • Water + Earth — Clay: emotional grounding, fertile manifestation.
  • Air + Earth — Dust: practical ideas, communication of tangible plans.

Protocol 4: Decanic & Dwan Attributions (Advanced)

Each zodiac sign divides into three decans (10° each), ruled by planetary sub-rulers. The Golden Dawn assigns Minor Arcana to decans; Major Arcana govern the sign as a whole. For precision timing:

  • Aries (Emperor): Decan 1 (0-10°) = Mars, Decan 2 (10-20°) = Sun, Decan 3 (20-30°) = Venus.
  • Taurus (Hierophant): Decan 1 = Venus, Decan 2 = Mercury, Decan 3 = Saturn.
  • Gemini (Lovers): Decan 1 = Mercury, Decan 2 = Venus, Decan 3 = Saturn.
  • Cancer (Chariot): Decan 1 = Moon, Decan 2 = Mars, Decan 3 = Jupiter.
  • Leo (Strength): Decan 1 = Sun, Decan 2 = Jupiter, Decan 3 = Mars.
  • Virgo (Hermit): Decan 1 = Mercury, Decan 2 = Saturn, Decan 3 = Venus.
  • Libra (Justice): Decan 1 = Venus, Decan 2 = Saturn, Decan 3 = Jupiter.
  • Scorpio (Death): Decan 1 = Mars, Decan 2 = Sun, Decan 3 = Venus.
  • Sagittarius (Temperance): Decan 1 = Jupiter, Decan 2 = Mars, Decan 3 = Sun.
  • Capricorn (Devil): Decan 1 = Saturn, Decan 2 = Venus, Decan 3 = Mercury.
  • Aquarius (Star): Decan 1 = Saturn, Decan 2 = Mercury, Decan 3 = Venus.
  • Pisces (Moon): Decan 1 = Jupiter, Decan 2 = Mars, Decan 3 = Moon.

Decanic Reading Protocol

Step 1: Note the exact degree of the querent's Sun (or relevant planet) in their natal chart.
Step 2: Identify the decan ruler for that degree.
Step 3: When the corresponding Major Arcana appears, the decan ruler shows the specific flavor of that card's energy for this person.
Example: Querent has Sun at 15° Leo (Decan 2 = Jupiter). Strength appears → the Strength energy manifests through Jupiter: expansion of confidence, generous leadership, teaching others.

Case Studies

Case Study 1: Career Crossroads — The Chariot + Wheel of Fortune

Querent: 34-year-old marketing director, considering job offer. Natal: Sun 15° Cancer, Moon 22° Scorpio, Saturn 29° Libra.

Spread: The Chariot (Cancer) + Wheel of Fortune (Jupiter) + Eight of Pentacles.

Analysis: The Chariot = Cancer (querent's Sun sign) — this is identity-level. Wheel of Fortune = Jupiter, transiting Jupiter was at 18° Taurus (querent's 11th house of career hopes). The combination: "Your identity (Cancer/Sun) is driving a career cycle change (Jupiter/11th house). The Eight of Pentacles confirms skill-building in the new role."

Outcome: Querent accepted. 6 months later, Jupiter entered Gemini (12th house) — restructuring began. The reading timed the window precisely.

Case Study 2: Relationship Timing — The Lovers + The Star

Querent: 28-year-old, single, asking "when will I meet someone?" Natal: Venus 5° Gemini, Jupiter 12° Aquarius.

Spread: The Lovers (Gemini/Mercury) + The Star (Aquarius/Uranus) + Two of Cups.

Analysis: The Lovers = Gemini (querent's Venus sign) — relationship energy is natal. The Star = Aquarius (querent's Jupiter sign) — expansion through unusual/future-oriented connections. Timing: Transiting Jupiter would enter Gemini in 14 months (conjunct natal Venus). Transiting Uranus in Taurus trine natal Jupiter.

Prediction: "Significant meeting during Jupiter in Gemini (May 2025 - June 2026), likely through social/tech circles (Aquarius/Uranus)."

Result: Met partner at a tech conference (Aquarius) in March 2025, during Jupiter's ingress to Gemini.

Case Study 3: Shadow Work — The Devil + The Tower

Querent: 42-year-old, stuck in addictive pattern. Natal: Pluto 28° Libra, Mars 15° Capricorn, Saturn 3° Cancer.

Spread: The Devil (Capricorn/Saturn) + The Tower (Mars) + Nine of Swords.

Analysis: The Devil = Capricorn (querent's Mars sign) — the addiction IS the martial drive misdirected into structure. The Tower = Mars (querent's Mars ruler) — sudden breakthrough is available but violent. Saturn in Cancer (4th house) = family root of pattern.

Protocol Applied: Mars-Saturn square in natal = the tension. The Devil (Saturn/Capricorn) shows the structure maintaining the trap. The Tower (Mars) shows the only exit: Mars-energy properly directed (exercise, combat sports, protective work).

Recommendation: 40-day Mars discipline (daily physical exertion at Mars hour) + Saturn banishing ritual (Saturday, Saturn hour).

Practice Exercises

Exercise 1: Personal Birth Card Calculation (30 min)

  1. Calculate your Birth Card: Add birth month + day + year digits until single digit (1-21) or 22 for The Fool. Example: July 15, 1990 = 7+1+5+1+9+9+0 = 32 = 3+2 = 5 → The Hierophant.
  2. Calculate your Shadow Card: Subtract birth card from 22. 22-5 = 17 → The Star.
  3. Find the planetary rulers: Hierophant = Taurus/Venus; Star = Aquarius/Uranus.
  4. Check your natal Venus and Uranus: house, sign, aspects. These reveal your core life dialectic: tradition vs. innovation, values vs. freedom.
  5. Journal: How do these themes play out in your life? When do you feel the Taurus pull? The Uranus push?

Exercise 2: Planetary Day Reading (Daily, 10 min)

  1. Each day corresponds to a planet: Sunday=Sun, Monday=Moon, Tuesday=Mars, Wednesday=Mercury, Thursday=Jupiter, Friday=Venus, Saturday=Saturn.
  2. Draw ONE Major Arcana. Note its planetary ruler.
  3. If the card's planet = day's planet: amplified message. If different: note the planetary relationship (sextile, square, trine, opposition).
  4. Example: Tuesday (Mars) + The Tower (Mars) = direct hit. Tuesday (Mars) + The Empress (Venus) = Mars-Venus dynamic: creative tension, passion vs. harmony.
  5. Track for 28 days (lunar cycle). Review patterns.

Exercise 3: Transit-Triggered Reading (Event-Based)

  1. Identify upcoming major transit: Jupiter return, Saturn square, Uranus opposition, Pluto transit.
  2. Find the Major Arcana for that planet (Wheel=Jupiter, World=Saturn, Fool=Uranus, Judgement=Pluto).
  3. When the transit goes exact (or within 1° orb), do a 3-card spread: The Transit Card + Two Supporting Cards.
  4. Interpret using the transit's house position in your natal chart.
  5. Document outcome at 30/90/180 days post-transit.

Exercise 4: Elemental Balance Audit (Weekly)

  1. Draw 4 cards (one per element): Fire, Water, Air, Earth Major Arcana representatives.
  2. Fire: Emperor/Strength/Tower/Sun/Judgement | Water: High Priestess/Chariot/Death/Hanged Man/Moon | Air: Fool/Magician/Lovers/Justice/Star | Earth: Empress/Hierophant/Hermit/Devil/World/Temperance.
  3. Note which element dominates your draw. That element needs attention or is overactive.
  4. Prescribe: If Fire dominant → grounding (Earth practices). If Water dominant → mental clarity (Air practices). If Air dominant → emotional processing (Water practices). If Earth dominant → inspiration/movement (Fire practices).

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the Golden Dawn tarot-astrology correspondences for the Major Arcana?

The Golden Dawn system assigns each Major Arcana card to a Hebrew letter, planet, or zodiac sign. The Fool=Uranus/Aleph, The Magician=Mercury/Beth, The High Priestess=Moon/Gimel, The Empress=Venus/Daleth, The Emperor=Aries/Heh, The Hierophant=Taurus/Vav, The Lovers=Gemini/Zayin, The Chariot=Cancer/Cheth, Strength=Leo/Teth, The Hermit=Virgo/Yod, Wheel of Fortune=Jupiter/Kaph, Justice=Libra/Lamed, The Hanged Man=Neptune/Mem, Death=Scorpio/Nun, Temperance=Sagittarius/Samekh, The Devil=Capricorn/Ayin, The Tower=Mars/Peh, The Star=Aquarius/Tzaddi, The Moon=Pisces/Qoph, The Sun=Sun/Resh, Judgement=Pluto/Shin, The World=Saturn/Tav.

How do I use tarot-astrology correspondences for timing in readings?

Each Major Arcana corresponds to planetary periods. The Sun=Sunday/summer/Leo season, The Moon=night/winter/Pisces season, Mercury cards=Wednesday/Gemini/Virgo periods, Venus cards=Friday/Taurus/Libra periods, Mars cards=Tuesday/Aries/Scorpio periods, Jupiter cards=Thursday/Sagittarius/Pisces periods, Saturn cards=Saturday/Capricorn/Aquarius periods. For precise timing, check the querent's natal transits to the corresponding planet.

What is the difference between Golden Dawn and Thoth tarot astrology correspondences?

The main difference: Crowley swapped Justice (XI) and Strength (VIII) in the Thoth deck to align with zodiacal order (Leo=Strength=8th sign, Libra=Justice=11th sign). Golden Dawn has Strength=VIII (Leo) and Justice=XI (Libra) — same zodiacal alignment but different numbering. Thoth also adds decanic attributions and elemental dignities. Choose one system and stay consistent.

Can I use these correspondences with any tarot deck?

Yes, but deck imagery matters. Waite-Smith decks follow Golden Dawn correspondences. Thoth decks follow Crowley's system. Marseille decks use earlier French correspondences (some differ). Modern decks vary. If the deck's imagery clearly depicts astrological symbols (e.g., The Star showing Aquarius water-bearer), follow the deck. If not, apply your chosen system consistently.

What if a card's planet is retrograde in the querent's chart?

Retrograde natal planets internalize the card's energy. The Magician (Mercury) with natal Mercury Rx = internalized willpower, communication challenges that become strengths through introspection. The Wheel (Jupiter Rx) = internal luck, philosophical review. The Tower (Mars Rx) = suppressed anger that explodes when triggered. Always note retrograde status in natal overlay.

How do I handle conflicting correspondences in a spread?

Conflicts = complexity, not error. The Devil (Capricorn/Saturn) + The Star (Aquarius/Uranus) = Saturn-Uranus tension: structure vs. liberation. Read the aspect between the planets: conjunction=fusion, sextile=opportunity, square=conflict requiring action, trine=flow, opposition=polarity to integrate. The cards show the planetary dialogue.
