Frequently Asked Questions

How does identity shift differ from affirmations?

Affirmations = PFC trying to override DMN (top-down, effortful, fails under stress). Identity shift = DMN rewiring (bottom-up, automatic after myelination). Affirmations = 'I want to be.' Identity shift = 'I am.' The DMN rewires through 21-day embodiment, not repetition of words.

How long does an identity shift take?

Minimum 21 days (14-day myelination + 7 integration). Full integration: identity alignment score ≥8/10 sustained 14+ days. Behavioral auto-alignment = actions flow from identity without willpower. The 14-day myelination cycle is the biological constraint.

Can I shift multiple identities at once?

No. The self-model (DMN) rewires as a unit. One identity per 21-day cycle. Attempting multiple = partial rewiring = cognitive dissonance. Sequential: one identity per 21-day cycle, 7-day buffer between cycles.

How do I know the identity shift worked?

Shift confirmed when: 1) Identity alignment score ≥8/10 ('I am someone who [new identity]') sustained 14+ days. 2) Behaviors flow automatically (no willpower). 3) Old trigger → new automatic response. 4) Identity alignment ≥8/10 sustained 14+ days. 5) Identity Verification Spread confirms.

What if the new identity feels fake?

"Fake" = PFC overriding DMN (top-down effort). The DMN hasn't rewired yet. The feeling of 'faking it' IS the rewiring process. Continue 21-day embodiment. If still fake after 21 days: wrong target identity. Re-audit. Choose different target.
