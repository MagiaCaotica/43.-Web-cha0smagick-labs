Wealth and Abundance

By Frater Alek0s ⬢ September 27, 2026 ⬢ 9 min read

Money has been a target in occult literature for centuries, and the reason is not mystical. Money is universal, quantifiable, and socially legible, which makes it an unusually clean symbol: nobody disagrees about what it means, everyone can see whether it arrived, and the outcome cannot be argued away. That is exactly the combination that makes money work attractive and money work dangerous. A money practice can be a disciplined piece of symbolic work with measurable habits built around it, or it can be a machine for producing confident predictions about outcomes nobody controls. This article describes the first kind, including the parts that require you to write down in advance what you will count as a result.

Money as a symbolic target, and why it keeps coming up

The attraction is legibility. A sigil for a vague sense of wellbeing produces vague evidence, and a sigil for a specific amount of money by a specific date produces a specific piece of evidence. Practitioners gravitate toward money because it is the one domain where their own outcome is legible to them. That is not a criticism of occult practice, it is a description of why the domain fills up with confident claims and few records.

The other half of the reason is that money touches identity. Financial position is read as evidence about a person, so a money working attracts projected meaning that a sigil for a lost key does not. In practice this shows up as escalating expectations between attempts, and it is the mechanism behind most failed abundance rituals. The symbol is fine. The expectation attached to it is the problem.

The command form against the request form

Sigils in this tradition encode imperatives rather than requests. The statement is written as an instruction to yourself, such as being the kind of person who has steady income, rather than as a petition addressed to something else. The grammatical difference sounds cosmetic and is not, because a request puts you in the position of a supplicant and a command puts you in the position of the one who instructs.

The practical consequence shows up during the charge. Asking for money produces straining, because the outcome is out of your hands and your attention registers the difference. Stating a condition of your own life produces something closer to recognition, and that state is the one that supports a sustained working. Grammatical mood is not a magic variable, but it tracks an attitude that does most of the practical work in this material.

741 and what the arithmetic is doing

The number circulates widely in abundance work because seven plus four plus one gives twelve, a figure that recurs across temperament charts and numerological systems. The relationship is real in the sense that the digits sum to twelve. Everything claimed beyond that is borrowed coherence rather than mechanism, and the honest description is that the practice borrows a numerological pattern instead of asserting a physical one.

This is a good example of how the material works in general. A resonance with a chart, a number that appears in several unrelated systems, a shape that recurs in old illustrations. None of these is evidence of influence and none is entirely arbitrary either. The defensible use of a pattern like this is as a focusing device that makes the intention concrete, which is a modest function and a real one.

Sigilisation as the standard method

Letter reduction, documented in Austin Osman Spare's work from the early 1900s, is the standard charge method in this current. You write the statement, remove vowels, assign shapes to consonants, drop duplicates, and join what remains into a figure. It is a compression technique and it works because a compact glyph proves simpler to carry in memory than a full sentence, not because letters carry occult properties.

That framing matters for money work in particular. If you believe the glyph is doing something, the drawing is where your attention goes and the rest of the practice becomes decoration. If you treat it as a device for carrying a statement, the drawing is bookkeeping and the attention stays on the statement. The second reading produces more consistent results, for the unexciting reason that it keeps your attention on the thing you actually wrote.

What a money working can honestly influence

It can install a routine. That is the strongest available claim and it is not trivial. A working that involves writing a statement, reducing it, sitting with an outcome you have already described, and logging the date takes fifteen minutes and creates a weekly appointment with your own finances that most people do not otherwise have.

It can alter what you notice. Writing a statement about steady income makes you aware of unpaid invoices, of conversations you have been avoiding about money, and of small amounts that leak. Nothing supernatural is required for that shift, and it is where the practical yield of most abundance practice comes from. What it cannot do is manufacture an amount of money from nothing, and any practitioner who claims otherwise is describing a coincidence that has not happened yet.

Building the statement before the glyph

One sentence, a subject and a condition, kept short enough to reduce cleanly. Being the kind of person who has three months of expenses held is a better target than having a lot of money, because it is a condition rather than a quantity and quantities invite comparison. Wanting more is a description of dissatisfaction, which sigilises into a request rather than a command.

Write it, date it, and keep it with the log rather than with the sigil. The paper is the specification and it is the only artefact that can be checked later. Practitioners who keep the glyph and discard the sentence end up unable to tell what any of their figures were for, which is why collections of money sigils tend to be decorative.

Choosing a window without inventing superstition

Timing choices are easy to over-engineer, and the over-engineering is usually a way of avoiding the working. Moon phases, planetary days, and specific hours all have defensible symbolic logic and no demonstrated causal one. A working performed on a Moon day when the target is about cycles, or during a hour you can actually keep free, is fine.

The test is whether the timing rule survives a month of inconvenient weekdays. If it requires you to be free at a specific hour on a specific day, it will fail most weeks, and a working you skip is worth less than a working you do badly on time. Pick one window, keep it, and let the symbol be a garnish rather than a prerequisite.

A six-week sequence

Weeks one and two, one sigil per week on a small target you would not mind failing at, plus the log. Weeks three and four, the same format on a slightly larger target, keeping the original statement style. Week five, no new sigils, review the log and count what actually happened. Week six, one sigil on the original target from week one, with the same wording, so the comparison is clean.

Six sigils in six weeks is a small amount and that is the point. Most abundance practice fails because the first attempt is aimed at something enormous, the evidence is too slow to arrive, and the practitioner concludes the method is broken. A small target produces a result inside the window, and a result inside the window is what keeps someone doing the method long enough to learn whether it works.

Writing down what counts as a result first

Before the charge, write the date you will check, the outcome you would count, and the outcome that would count as partial. Ambiguity here is the mechanism behind most false confidence, because a vaguely worded target can be confirmed by almost any later event once you have decided it counts.

Then write the result whatever it is, including nothing, and keep the log even after you lose interest. The value of the log appears around attempt twenty, when you can finally see your own hit rate and your own preparation. Before that point the practice is producing atmosphere, and no amount of additional technique substitutes for the record that would let you tell the difference.

Where abundance practice turns into something else

It turns when the sigil becomes a way of avoiding a decision. A working about a business problem substitutes for the conversation you need to have. A working about a relationship substitutes for the boundary you need to set. In both cases the ritual provides the feeling of having acted, which is more comfortable and considerably less effective than the action.

The second turn is the confidence spiral. Three small successes produce certainty about a large target, the large target produces no result inside the window, and the certainty is retro-fitted onto the small successes rather than revised. This is the most common failure pattern in money work and it is entirely avoidable with the log, because the log is the thing that would catch it.

The ethics of a money working

Nothing in this material makes anyone worse off, and the requests people bring are overwhelmingly ordinary: rent, invoices, a job, a debt. The ethical problems are adjacent. Promising another person a financial outcome you cannot deliver is a straightforward harm. Working on someone else's money without their knowledge treats them as material rather than as a participant.

The second concern is self-directed. A practice whose entire structure is about believing you can override outcomes is not a good frame for making financial decisions, because the same confidence applied elsewhere is how people overleverage. Keep money workings small, keep them away from borrowing decisions, and use the money you already have to make the routine rather than to finance the ritual.

When to stop, and what to keep

Stop when the practice has become more absorbing than the finances it addresses, when you notice yourself escalating targets to justify prior attempts, or when the sigil has become something you feel is running you rather than something you did. Any of those is a sufficient reason, and none of them requires a dramatic event to justify.

Keep the log. Not the sigils, which are handles and can be discarded or buried, and not the certainty, which is the part that tends to be wrong. The log is thirty lines that tell you: what you did, what you expected and what followed, and that is what separates a practice that taught you something and one that only confirmed what you wanted to believe.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a sigil actually attract money?

Not directly, and no practitioner has evidence that it can. What a money sigil can do reliably is create a recurring appointment with your own finances and make you more alert to money-shaped opportunities you were ignoring. That indirect effect is real and worth having. expecting one particular amount by one particular date out of a glyph is a request for coincidence that you will then credit to the working.

Why do abundance sigils use commands instead of asks?

Because a request puts you in the position of asking someone else, and straining is the state you want to avoid during a charge. A command about your own condition, phrased as something you are instructing yourself to be, produces recognition rather than supplication. The mood of the sentence tracks an attitude that does most of the practical work in this material.

What is the significance of 741?

Seven plus four plus one comes to twelve, a number that turns up across numerological and temperament systems, and that resonance is the entire basis for its use in abundance work. It functions as a focusing device that makes an intention concrete. Nothing about the digits indicates a physical mechanism, and anyone claiming otherwise is selling rather than describing.

How many money sigils should I make?

Six in six weeks is plenty, and small targets matter more than quantity. The most common failure is a first attempt aimed at something enormous that produces no result inside the window, followed by the conclusion that the method is broken. A small target that resolves quickly teaches you the same thing and keeps you practising long enough to find out.

Should I use a prosperity sigil for a business problem?

Only if the problem is yours to decide. Where the obstacle is an unpaid invoice, a contract you have not chased, or a conversation about money, the sigil is likely substituting for the action. If the problem is the market, the numbers, or somebody else's decision, a working has nothing to act on. Keep it away from borrowing decisions as well, since the material encourages a confidence that is unhelpful there.
