Moon magic rewards consistency more than intensity. A single working at the perfect phase beats a dozen rushed ones at the wrong time — which is why a yearly plan beats a nightly scramble. The Lunar Phase Calculator app turns the entire 2026 lunar calendar into a planner you can check in seconds.

Why Plan a Lunar Year

There are 12 or 13 lunar months per solar year — 13 in 2026. Each month runs new moon to new moon (~29.5 days) and contains four anchor phases: new, first quarter, full, last quarter. Planning a year means you never miss a new moon for beginnings or a full moon for charging, and you stop casting banishings on the wrong half of the cycle.

  • Set all your intentions at the new moon, when the sky is dark and the cycle is empty.
  • Do growth and attraction work during the waxing half (new to full).
  • Peak charging, charging talismans and sigils, at the full moon.
  • Release, banish and cut cords during the waning half (full to new).
  • Rest, scry and do shadow work at the dark moon just before the new moon.

The 13 Lunar Months

Because 2026 has 13 lunations, you get one extra full cycle to work with. Number them month 1 through 13 and give each a theme: money in month 1, love in month 2, career in month 3, health in month 4, and so on. The theme repeats every year, which turns a yearly planner into a reusable ritual system.

A Simple Planning Method

  1. Write your 3-5 yearly goals as phrases, not spells (e.g. 'steady freelance income').
  2. Assign each goal one lunar month as its theme month.
  3. Inside that month: new moon sets the intention, waxing builds it, full moon charges it, waning releases it.
  4. Block the four anchor dates into your calendar at the start of the month.
  5. Keep one intention per phase — do not overload the new moon with ten goals.

Tracking Results

Log four things after every working: the phase, the exact intention, the date, and the outcome. Over three months, patterns appear — money work landing consistently at waxing, or relationship readings only clarifying at the full moon. The Lunar Phase Calculator app keeps the phase dates and a journal in one place, offline, with no account.

Void of Course

The moon is 'void of course' for a few hours between its last major aspect and the next sign change. Tradition says nothing started then comes to anything. Check the void window before scheduling important workings — the app flags it, so you can plan around it instead of fighting it.

Common Mistakes

  • Casting growth spells in the waning half and wondering why they stall.
  • Scheduling every working at the full moon and creating a bottleneck.
  • Ignoring the void of course windows entirely.
  • Starting a new intention at the full moon instead of the new moon.
  • Never logging results, so no patterns ever become visible.

FAQ

Do I need to follow the moon strictly?

No — the moon is a timing aid, not a requirement. Use it when a working can wait a few days; cast urgent work whenever you must, and simply log which phase you used.

What if I miss my new moon window?

The 48-hour window after the exact new moon is generous. If you miss it entirely, wait for the next month's new moon rather than forcing the intention mid-cycle.
