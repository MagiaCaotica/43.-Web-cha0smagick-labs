Pendulum Healing & Chakra Balancing: Complete Energy Work Guide

July 30, 2026Frater Alek0sPendulum Divination

Pendulum healing combines the ancient art of dowsing with modern energy work to detect and clear energetic blockages. Practitioners use the pendulum as a diagnostic tool — its movement reveals where energy flows freely and where it stagnates. When combined with chakra knowledge this technique becomes a complete system for energy maintenance.

The 7 Chakras and Pendulum Diagnosis

Hold your pendulum about 2 inches above each chakra point. A healthy balanced chakra produces steady clockwise movement. Stillness irregular movement or counterclockwise spinning may indicate blockage or overactivity.

  • Root Chakra (Base of Spine): Grounding security survival. Pendulum over tailbone.
  • Sacral Chakra (Below Navel): Creativity sexuality emotions. Pendulum over lower abdomen.
  • Solar Plexus (Above Navel): Personal power confidence. Pendulum over stomach area.
  • Heart Chakra (Center of Chest): Love compassion connection. Pendulum over heart.
  • Throat Chakra (Throat): Communication expression truth. Pendulum over throat.
  • Third Eye (Between Eyebrows): Intuition insight vision. Pendulum over forehead.
  • Crown Chakra (Top of Head): Spirituality connection. Pendulum above head.

Clearing Blockages with Pendulum

Once you have identified blocked or imbalanced chakras hold the pendulum over the area and visualize bright healing light — red for root orange for sacral yellow for solar plexus green for heart blue for throat indigo for third eye violet for crown. Program the pendulum by stating: "Swing clockwise to clear." Let it spin until the movement becomes smooth and regular. This can take 1-5 minutes per chakra. Take deep breaths throughout.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a pendulum detect energy blockages?
Yes. Hold the pendulum over each chakra point. If it swings in wide circles the chakra is open and balanced. If it is still or swings erratically there may be a blockage. Pendulum practitioners use this for diagnostic energy work.
How do I use a pendulum for chakra healing?
Hold the pendulum over each chakra starting from the root (base of spine) moving up to the crown (top of head). Note the movement at each point. Spend extra time over blocked chakras visualizing healing light. Some practitioners program their pendulum to spin clockwise for "clearing."
Is pendulum healing scientifically proven?
No controlled studies have validated pendulum healing. However the practice is valued in many energy work traditions. The pendulum may serve as a biofeedback tool — your unconscious mind communicates through subtle movements. The healing effect comes from focused intention and relaxation.
Can I use a digital pendulum for healing?
Digital pendulums are better suited for divination than energy healing. Physical pendulums have a tactile quality important for body-based energy work. Use our free Digital Pendulum tool for yes/no guidance and a physical pendulum for chakra work.
