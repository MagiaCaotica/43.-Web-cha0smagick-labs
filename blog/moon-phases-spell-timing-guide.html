Eclipse Protocols: The Wild Cards

Eclipses are accelerated nodes — they compress months of karmic unfolding into minutes. Handle with extreme caution.

Solar Eclipse (New Moon)

  • Energy: Radical new beginnings, fate interventions, revelation of hidden truths
  • Window: Exact eclipse moment ± 4 hours
  • Best For: Life-path changes, destiny work, breaking generational patterns, major initiations
  • Protocol: Do NOT cast standard new moon spells. Instead: meditate, divine, receive. The eclipse shows you what to initiate. Write insights immediately post-eclipse. Act on them at the next New Moon.
  • Warning: Effects manifest over 6 months. Do not force.

Lunar Eclipse (Full Moon)

  • Energy: Culmination, emotional release, shadow exposure, relationship reckoning
  • Window: Exact eclipse moment ± 4 hours
  • Best For: Deep release, shadow integration, ending karmic cycles, ancestral healing
  • Protocol: Do NOT charge tools or manifest. Instead: witness, release, let go. Write what you're releasing. Burn safely. The eclipse takes what you offer.
  • Warning: Emotional volatility peaks. Avoid binding spells.

Eclipse Season Strategy

Eclipses occur in pairs (solar + lunar) twice yearly, ~2 weeks apart. The period between them is the "eclipse corridor" — volatile, revelatory. Best practice: observe, record, do not force. Resume active spellcasting 3 days after the lunar eclipse.

Spell Recipes by Phase: Exact Protocols

🌑 New Moon: Sigil of Intention

  1. Timing: Exact New Moon moment ± 90 min (check timezone)
  2. Tools: White candle, black ink, parchment, sigil method of choice
  3. Protocol: Enter gnosis (inhibitory: breath hold 30s × 3). Write intention as present-tense statement. Remove vowels/repeating letters. Design sigil. Charge with directed gaze + orgasm/breath/fear. Burn or bury.
  4. Follow-up: Feed daily for 14 days (Waxing phases). Release at Full Moon.

🌒 Waxing Crescent: Momentum Candle

  1. Timing: First visible crescent, Mars hour preferred
  2. Tools: Green candle (growth), red candle (action), cinnamon oil, lodestone
  3. Protocol: Dress candles with oil. Place lodestone between. Light green then red. Speak: "As this flame grows, so grows my will." Visualize first concrete step. Let burn completely.
  4. Follow-up: Carry lodestone. Touch when taking action.

🌓 First Quarter: Obstacle Breaker

  1. Timing: Exact First Quarter ± 90 min, Mars hour (Tuesday ideal)
  2. Tools: Black candle, red thread, nail, paper, fireproof bowl
  3. Protocol: Write obstacle on paper. Pierce with nail saying "This obstacle is pierced." Wrap with red thread 9x. Burn in black candle flame. Scatter ashes at crossroads.
  4. Follow-up: Take physical action toward solution within 24 hours.

🌔 Waxing Gibbous: Clarity Mirror

  1. Timing: Mercury hour (Wednesday ideal), 2 days before Full Moon
  2. Tools: Black mirror or bowl of water, blue candle, mugwort
  3. Protocol: Light candle. Burn mugwort. Gaze into mirror. Ask: "What must I refine?" Record visions. Adjust sigil/method accordingly.
  4. Follow-up: Implement one refinement immediately.

🌕 Full Moon: Major Manifestation & Tool Charging

  1. Timing: Exact Full Moon ± 2 hours (widest window)
  2. Tools: All tools to charge, silver/gold candles, moon water vessel, crystals
  3. Protocol: Arrange tools in circle. Light candles. Draw down moon: raise arms, inhale silver light, exhale into tools. Speak charge: "By lunar light, you are awakened." Leave overnight. Bottle moon water.
  4. Follow-up: Use charged tools immediately. Moon water keeps 28 days refrigerated.

🌖 Waning Gibbous: Gratitude Anchor

  1. Timing: Venus hour (Friday ideal), 2 days post-Full
  2. Tools: Pink/green candle, rose petals, honey, journal
  3. Protocol: Light candles. List 13 things manifested this cycle. Read aloud. Drop rose petals in honey. Eat one petal per item. Seal jar. Keep on altar.
  4. Follow-up: Share one manifestation story publicly (teaching = anchoring).

🌗 Last Quarter: Cord Cutting

  1. Timing: Exact Last Quarter ± 90 min, Saturn hour (Saturday ideal)
  2. Tools: Two black candles, red thread, scissors, paper, black salt
  3. Protocol: Write what to release on paper. Place between candles. Tie thread between candles representing cord. Visualize cord. Cut with scissors. Burn paper. Sprinkle black salt on ashes.
  4. Follow-up: Physical cleanup: delete contacts, throw objects, change locks as needed.

🌑 Dark Moon: Dream Incubation

  1. Timing: Night before New Moon, Moon hour (Monday ideal)
  2. Tools: Mugwort pillow, dream journal, blue candle, amethyst
  3. Protocol: Place mugwort in pillowcase. Amethyst under pillow. Light candle. Ask: "Show me what the next cycle requires." Sleep. Record immediately on waking.
  4. Follow-up: Translate dream symbols into next New Moon intentions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cast spells during a void-of-course Moon?
Avoid it for important work. Void-of-course periods (when the Moon makes no major aspects before changing signs) produce unreliable, "floaty" results. Exception: purely devotional, meditative, or divinatory work. Check daily VOC windows — they last minutes to hours.
How long does moon phase energy actually last?
Each phase spans ~3.5 days (84 hours). The exact phase moment is peak (±90 min optimal window). The day before and after carry ~70% potency. The middle day is ~50%. Plan major workings for the exact moment; minor workings anytime in phase.
What if I need emergency protection during the "wrong" phase?
Urgent protection always overrides timing. A spell cast with genuine necessity in the "wrong" phase outperforms a casual spell in the "right" phase. Use Mars hour + Saturn hour + personal Lunar Return for emergency work regardless of collective phase.
Do I need to calculate everything manually?
No. Use the Lunar Phase Calculator App ($3.99) for exact phase moments, void-of-course, planetary hours, and mansion data for your location. The free Lunar Phase tool gives basic phase data.
Can I combine multiple intentions in one lunar cycle?
Maximum 3 distinct intentions per cycle (one per decan/10° segment). More dilutes focus. If you have 5+ goals, prioritize top 3 this cycle, next 3 next cycle. The 30-day program is designed for exactly 3 intentions.
What about Southern Hemisphere practitioners?
Lunar phases are identical globally (same illumination %). Seasons are reversed. Adjust elemental correspondences: waxing = building (Fire/Air) universally; waning = releasing (Earth/Water) universally. Seasonal festivals (Sabbats) flip; lunar phases do not.
How do I handle Mercury retrograde during a waxing phase?
Mercury retrograde in waxing = review/refine, not initiate. Use Waxing Gibbous (refinement phase) for retrograde work: review contracts, edit writings, re-connect, re-pair. Avoid new launches. New Moon during Mercury retrograde = set intentions for post-retrograde launch.
Can lunar magic work for non-believers / skeptics?
The mechanism is psychological + statistical: timing actions to natural rhythms increases consistency, reduces decision fatigue, and leverages circadian/circalunar biology. Treat as a productivity framework if preferred. Results follow action + timing regardless of belief.
What's the difference between this and "moon manifesting" TikTok trends?
Trends oversimplify: "write wish on paper at Full Moon." This guide provides: exact phase moments, planetary hour overlays, 28 mansions, eclipse protocols, troubleshooting, case studies, and a structured 30-day program. Precision is the difference between lottery ticket and targeted operation.
