Phase 1: Design Specification — The Blueprint

A servitor without a written specification is a hallucination. The specification document is your contract with the construct — it defines what it is, what it does, how it lives, and how it dies. Every field below is mandatory. If you cannot fill a field, the servitor is not ready for birth.

The Servitor Specification Document (One Page, Mandatory)

SERVITOR SPEC v1.0 ================== NAME: ________________________________________ DATE CREATED: _________________________________ 1. PURPOSE (Mandate — ONE measurable sentence): _______________________________________________ _______________________________________________ 2. PARAMETERS (When/How it operates): Trigger: _______________________________________ Frequency: ____________________________________ Method: _______________________________________ Scope: ________________________________________ 3. CONSTRAINTS (What it CANNOT do): _______________________________________________ _______________________________________________ 4. FEEDING SCHEDULE: Daily (Attention): ______ min, Method: ___________ Weekly (Energy): ______ min, Method: ____________ Monthly (Data): _______ Metrics: ________________ Optional (Blood/Sexual): ______ Frequency: _______ 5. HEALTH METRICS (How to measure): Primary KPI: ___________________________________ Secondary: _____________________________________ Energy Baseline (1-10): ____ Anomaly Threshold: _____________________________ 6. TERMINATION TRIGGER: [ ] Mandate fulfilled: _________________________ [ ] Date: _____________________________________ [ ] Condition: _________________________________ 7. EMERGENCY OFF-SWITCH: Verbal Command: "I REVOKE [NAME]. TERMINATE NOW." Sigil Burn: Immediate. Banish: LBRP. Ground: Salt/Water/Sleep.

Dual Sigil Generation (Mandatory)

  1. Identity Sigil: From "NAME + PURPOSE" via Chaos Sigil Generator. Anchors the servitor's existence.
  2. Command Sigil: From mandate sentence via Spare method (remove vowels, dedupe consonants, abstract glyph). Anchors the specific intent.
  3. Linking: Place both on charging plate. Visualize connection line. Charge together.

Housing (The Servitor's Address)

  • Physical: Small box, jar, or dedicated altar tile. Sigil visible. Kept in fixed location.
  • Digital: Encrypted folder with: sigil PNG/SVG, mandate.txt, feeding_log.csv, health_log.csv, spec.pdf.
  • Rule: Housing never moves without formal relocation ritual. Housing = address. No address = no delivery.

Phase 2: Birth — Charging & Activation

Birth is the moment the specification becomes a live construct. It requires gnosis (altered state), dual-sigil charging, verbal declaration, and sealing. Do not rush — failed birth = stillborn servitor that drains without output.

Pre-Birth Checklist

  • [ ] Spec document complete & printed
  • [ ] Dual sigils generated, printed, on charging plate
  • [ ] Housing ready (physical + digital)
  • [ ] Feeding schedule in calendar (recurring events)
  • [ ] Health dashboard created (spreadsheet/app)
  • [ ] Emergency off-switch memorized

Phase 3: Service — Feeding, Monitoring, Maintenance

Service is the working life of the servitor. It runs on the feeding schedule you defined. Missed feeds = parameter drift. 3 missed feeds = mandatory review. The servitor has no autonomy to request food — you are the operator.

Daily Feed (5 Minutes) — Attention

  1. Open housing / digital folder
  2. Read mandate aloud
  3. Log one action taken toward mandate today
  4. Rate subjective energy (1-10)
  5. Note anomalies (unintended effects, synchronicities, blocks)

Weekly Feed (15 Minutes) — Energy

  1. Gnosis induction: 4-7-8 breath ×3, anchor phrase
  2. Sigil gaze: 5 min on identity + command sigils
  3. Mandate restatement aloud
  4. Review weekly metrics: mandate progress %, anomaly count, dream log summary
  5. Adjust parameters if progress stalled 2+ weeks

Monthly Feed (30 Minutes) — Data + Deep Charge

  1. Full metrics dump: export all trackers (income, traffic, health, etc.)
  2. Feed data to servitor: visualize metrics flowing into housing
  3. Deep charge: 15 min gnosis (exhaustion/trance/sexual/fear) + both sigils
  4. Monthly audit: Progress %, Anomaly trend, Dream pattern, PSI GYM correlation
  5. Decision: Continue / Re-parameter / Re-charge / Terminate

Parameter Adjustment Protocol

If mandate progress stalls 2+ weeks:

  1. Run Clarifier: Is mandate still aligned? (Use tarot Clarifier spread)
  2. Check feeding compliance: Any missed feeds?
  3. Check PSI GYM: Is hit rate dropping? (Channel noise)
  4. Check dreams: Threat/neutral = drift. Ally/tool = functioning.
  5. Adjust ONE parameter at a time. Log change. Monitor 1 week.

Phase 4: Dissolution — Safe Termination & Emergency Banishment

Every servitor must end. A servitor without an end is a parasite. The off-switch is not optional — it's the most important part of the design.

Standard Termination (Mandate Fulfilled / Time Expired / Condition Met)

  1. Formal Revocation: Stand at housing. Aloud: "I, [YOUR NAME], revoke [SERVITOR NAME]. Your mandate is complete. Return to source. You are released."
  2. Sigil Destruction: Burn physical sigils completely. Ash to running water or crossroads. Digital: secure delete (7-pass overwrite) or encrypt + lose key.
  3. Energy Return: Stand. Visualize servitor's energy as light returning to your solar plexus. Do not keep — return to your central channel.
  4. Banish: LBRP, Star Ruby, Gnostic Pentagram, or your tradition's equivalent.
  5. Ground: Salt bath or shower (visualize draining). Drink 500ml water + pinch salt. Eat protein. Sleep 8h minimum.
  6. Journal: Log: Termination date, final mandate %, total days active, total feeds, anomalies resolved, lessons learned.

Emergency Termination (Rogue / Parasitic / Drift / Unintended Harm)

Triggers — IMMEDIATE ACTION REQUIRED:

  • Servitor acts outside parameters (unauthorized actions)
  • Energy drain >15% daily baseline (chronic fatigue, headaches, insomnia)
  • Dream reports: Threat / Attack / Parasitic (feeding on you)
  • Unintended consequences manifesting (harm to others, financial loss, relationship damage)
  • Parameter drift: Servitor redefines its own mandate
  • PSI GYM hit rate crashes + servitor active = channel hijack
EMERGENCY TERMINATION SEQUENCE (Execute in order, no hesitation): 1. VERBAL COMMAND (aloud, authoritative): "I AM THE OPERATOR. [NAME] IS TERMINATED NOW." 2. SIGIL BURN: Immediate. Physical → fire. Digital → secure delete NOW. 3. BANISH: Full LBRP + Middle Pillar (or your equivalent). No shortcuts. 4. SPACE CLEANSE: Smoke (sage/palo santo), salt perimeter, ventilate 10 min. 5. PERSONAL CLEANSE: Salt bath 20 min, 1L water + electrolytes, 8h sleep. 6. QUARANTINE: No magical work 72 hours. Monitor energy, dreams, health. 7. INCIDENT LOG: Date, trigger, actions taken, recovery timeline.

Post-Termination Quarantine (72 Hours)

  • No magical work. No servitor creation. No charging.
  • Monitor: Energy levels, sleep quality, dream content, physical symptoms.
  • If symptoms persist >7 days: seek experienced practitioner / medical eval.
NEVER reuse a terminated sigil. The charge pattern is contaminated. Generate fresh sigils for every new servitor. This is not superstition — it's data hygiene.

Advanced: Compound Servitors, Egregore Seeding & Codex Chaoticus

Compound Servitors (2-3 Archetypes)

For objectives requiring multiple functions that must coordinate.

  • Magician + Chariot: Manifestation + timeline defense. "Build the asset AND protect the deadline."
  • High Priestess + Hermit: Intuition + deep research. "Find the variable I cannot see."
  • Empress + Temperance: Growth + calibration. "Scale without breaking."
  • Death + Star: End + guide. "Clean transition to next phase."
  • Devil + Tower: Shadow exposure + demolition. "Burn the false structure."

Protocol: Create each as separate servitor with own spec, sigils, housing. Shared mandate clause: "Coordinates with [Other Name]." Shared feeding calendar. You are the coordination layer — they do not communicate directly.

Egregore Seeding (From Servitor to Group Entity)

A servitor can become an egregore if:

  1. Mandate expands to collective benefit
  2. Multiple operators adopt it (shared off-switch = consensus required)
  3. Feeding becomes group ritual
  4. Housing becomes shared space (physical or digital)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most common reason servitors fail?

Skipping the dissolution/off-switch design. A servitor without a defined termination trigger runs until it decays unpredictably — draining energy, drifting parameters, or going rogue. Always design the off-switch before birth.

How do I know my servitor is actually working vs. placebo?

Track measurable outputs: mandate progress %, PSI GYM hit rate correlation, dream log pattern (ally/tool vs threat), anomaly count. If mandate progresses + hit rate rises + dreams show ally/tool = working. If only subjective feeling = placebo.

Can I create a servitor for someone else?

Yes, but you remain the operator. The servitor answers to YOU. The target receives the effect; you hold the off-switch. Never create a servitor that answers to another person — that's an egregore, not a servitor, and requires group consensus.

What's the difference between a servitor and an egregore?

Servitor = single operator, specific task, defined lifespan, personal off-switch. Egregore = group-created, collective purpose, potentially indefinite, requires group consensus to terminate. Servitors are tools; egregores are entities.

How often should I feed a servitor?

Daily attention feed (5 min review/log) + Weekly energy feed (gnosis + sigil) + Monthly data feed (metrics dump). Blood/sexual feed optional monthly for high-output servitors. Missed feeds = parameter drift. 3 missed feeds = review required.
