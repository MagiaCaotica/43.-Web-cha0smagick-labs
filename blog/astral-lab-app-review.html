What Is Astral Lab?

Astral Lab is a one-time-purchase natal chart app for Android. You enter your birth date, time, and location once, and it computes your full birth chart — all planets, houses, and aspects — completely offline. No subscription. No account. No data collection.

For $6.99 you get: your complete natal chart, the Big Three highlighted at a glance, synastry compatibility between two charts, and transit tracking for planning. It is the closest thing to a professional astrologer's software that fits in your pocket — and it works on airplane mode.

Reading Your Chart

Every birth chart is a snapshot of the sky at your exact birth moment. The planets (Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Pluto) each sit in a zodiac sign and a house. The chart answers three questions: who you are (planets in signs), where you express it (houses), and how it plays out (aspects between planets).

Astral Lab renders this as an interactive wheel. Tap any planet to see its sign, house, and a plain-language interpretation. No ephemeris tables required — the app does the calculation for you.

The Big Three: Sun, Moon & Rising

Your Big Three is the shortcut everyone uses to describe your chart: the Sun (core identity), the Moon (emotional nature), and the Ascendant/Rising (the mask you show the world). The Moon and Rising require your exact birth time — the Ascendant changes roughly every two hours, so a wrong time gives you a wrong Rising sign.

Astral Lab makes your Big Three the first thing you see when you open the app, so you never have to dig through the full wheel to answer the most common astrology question: “What's my Big Three?”

Houses and Aspects

Beyond the Big Three, the twelve houses map your chart onto life areas: money (2nd), communication (3rd), home (4th), relationships (7th), career (10th), and so on. Aspects — conjunction, opposition, square, trine, sextile — show how planets interact.

Beginners often skip houses and aspects because they seem complicated. Astral Lab labels each house and aspect in plain language, so a square between Mars and Saturn reads as “frustration that rewards discipline” instead of a cryptic glyph.

Saturn Return

The Saturn return — when transiting Saturn returns to the position it held at your birth, around ages 28–30 and again at 58–60 — is the most famous astrological life milestone. Astral Lab's transit tracker shows when yours is approaching, what it means, and what to do with it.
