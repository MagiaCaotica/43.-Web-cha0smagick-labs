Planetary Transits Guide: How Current Planets Affect Your Daily Life

July 30, 2026Frater Alek0sAstrology

Your birth chart is fixed — a snapshot of the sky at your birth moment. But the planets keep moving. Planetary transits are the ongoing relationship between the current sky and your natal chart. They explain why some weeks feel effortless and others feel like pushing a boulder uphill. Understanding transits transforms astrology from a personality description into a practical daily tool.

How Transits Work

When a transiting planet forms an aspect to a planet or sensitive point in your birth chart it activates that area of your life. The nature of the transit depends on which planet is moving which natal planet it is touching and what kind of aspect it forms.

  • Conjunction (0°): Intensification — the transiting planet merges energy with your natal planet.
  • Trine (120°): Flow — easy supportive energy. Opportunities open effortlessly.
  • Sextile (60°): Opportunity — requires a small effort to activate but very supportive.
  • Square (90°): Tension — challenges obstacles friction. Difficult but promotes growth.
  • Opposition (180°): Awareness — external events mirror internal conflicts. Relationship-focused.

The Planets as Transiting Forces

🌙 Moon Transits (Hours)

The Moon moves quickly transiting each sign in about 2.5 days. Moon transits affect mood — you may feel unusually emotional or sensitive when the Moon aspects your natal Moon or Sun. Track Moon transits for daily emotional awareness.

☿ Mercury Transits (Days to Weeks)

Mercury transits affect communication thinking and travel. A Mercury transit to your natal Mercury sharpens your mind. Mercury retrograde periods affect everyone but hit harder if Mercury is aspecting personal planets.

♀ Venus Transits (Days to Weeks)

Venus transits bring love beauty and pleasure. When Venus trines your natal Venus you feel attractive social and magnetic. When Venus squares your Venus relationship friction may surface.

♂ Mars Transits (Weeks)

Mars transits energize — sometimes aggressively. Mars transiting your Sun boosts confidence and drive. Mars transiting your Moon may cause irritability. Channel Mars energy into physical activity and assertive action.

♃ Jupiter Transits (Months)

Jupiter brings expansion luck and opportunity. Jupiter transits to personal planets are generally beneficial — doors open optimism rises and things feel easier. The shadow side: overconfidence and excess. Jupiter takes about 12 years to circle the zodiac spending about 1 year in each sign.

♄ Saturn Transits (Years)

Saturn transits are the most significant for personal growth. Saturn demands discipline responsibility and authenticity. Saturn transits to personal planets can feel heavy — but they build lasting structures. Saturn takes 29.5 years to complete one orbit. The famous Saturn Return happens around ages 27-31.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a planetary transit in astrology?
A transit occurs when a planet in the current sky forms an aspect to a planet or point in your birth chart. For example when current Jupiter aligns with your natal Sun you are experiencing a Jupiter transit to your Sun.
How long do planetary transits last?
Moon: hours. Mercury Venus Sun: days to weeks. Mars: weeks. Jupiter: months. Saturn: 2.5 years (Saturn return lasts about 2.5 years). Uranus Neptune Pluto: years to decades.
What is a Saturn Return?
Saturn returns to the position it was at your birth approximately every 29.5 years. The first Saturn Return (ages 27-31) is famous as a period of major life restructuring. It forces you to assess whether you are living authentically.
Which transits are most important to track?
Saturn transits (life lessons and restructuring) Jupiter transits (opportunity and expansion) and outer planet transits to personal planets (Sun Moon Mercury Venus Mars) which indicate major life shifts.
