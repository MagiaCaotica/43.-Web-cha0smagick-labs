Your birth chart is fixed — a snapshot of the sky at your birth moment. But the planets keep moving. Planetary transits are the ongoing relationship between the current sky and your natal chart. They explain why some weeks feel effortless and others feel like pushing a boulder uphill. Understanding transits transforms astrology from a personality description into a practical daily tool for timing decisions, navigating challenges, and recognizing opportunities.

How Transits Work: The Mechanics

When a transiting planet forms an aspect to a planet or sensitive point in your birth chart, it activates that area of your life. The nature of the transit depends on four factors:

  1. Which planet is transiting — its archetypal meaning (Saturn = structure/limits, Jupiter = expansion/opportunity)
  2. Which natal planet it's touching — the part of your psyche being activated
  3. What kind of aspect it forms — the quality of the interaction (easy vs. challenging)
  4. Which houses are involved — the life areas affected (natal house = where you feel it, transiting house = where action originates)

Advanced Techniques

Transit Stacking: When Multiple Transits Converge

Power periods occur when 2+ transits hit the same natal planet/house simultaneously. Examples:

  • Jupiter trine Sun + Mars trine Sun = "Golden window" for action + expansion (1-2 weeks)
  • Saturn square Moon + Pluto square Moon = Deep emotional restructuring period (months)
  • Jupiter conjunct Venus + Venus trine Neptune = Relationship/creative peak (weeks)

Progressed Moon + Transits

Progressed Moon (~1 month per degree) acts as a "trigger" for slower transits. When progressed Moon aspects a natal planet that's also receiving a transit, the transit manifests concretely.

Solar Arc Directions + Transits

Solar arc (1° per year) to natal planets often coincides with major transit manifestations, providing confirmation.

FAQ: Planetary Transits

How do I know which transits are affecting me right now?
Use the Astral Lab app or Natal Chart Calculator with your exact birth data (date, time, location). Enable transit notifications for real-time alerts.
What's the difference between a transit and a progression?
Transits = actual current planetary positions aspecting your natal chart (external timing). Progressions = symbolic movement (1 day = 1 year) showing internal evolution. Transits trigger events; progressions show readiness.
Do transits cause events or just reflect them?
Both. Transits create the energetic field/archetypal pressure; your consciousness chooses the specific manifestation. Same transit can manifest as external event OR internal shift depending on awareness level.
How accurate are transit predictions?
Archetypal themes are 90%+ predictable (e.g., Saturn square Venus = relationship testing). Specific events vary based on free will, consciousness level, and supporting transits. Timing is precise (±days for fast planets, ±weeks for slow).
What if I don't know my birth time?
Use noon as default for planetary positions (accurate for all planets except Moon). House cusps and Ascendant will be unknown, so focus on planet-to-planet transits (Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn) which don't require houses.
Can I use transits for financial trading?
Some traders use Jupiter/Saturn cycles, Mercury retrograde, and lunar phases for timing. Requires extensive backtesting. Not financial advice. Start with paper tracking for 6+ months before risking capital.
What are "transit returns" and why do they matter?
A return = transiting planet conjunct its natal position. Solar Return (yearly), Lunar Return (monthly), Mercury Return (yearly), Venus Return (yearly), Mars Return (2-yearly), Jupiter Return (12-yearly), Saturn Return (29.5-yearly). Each marks a new cycle in that planet's domain.
How do I handle multiple difficult transits at once?
Prioritize: 1) Saturn/Pluto transits (structural/deep), 2) Mars transits (acute), 3) Mercury/Venus (manageable). Focus energy on the slowest/most impactful. Use faster transits for small wins. Maintain daily grounding practices.
