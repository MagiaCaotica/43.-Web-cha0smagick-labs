Phase 1: The Container — Pre-Session Safety Infrastructure

The ouija board is a circuit. Like any circuit, it needs proper insulation, grounding, and circuit breakers. The "container" is your pre-session safety infrastructure — the insulation that prevents leakage, the grounding that prevents shock, the breaker that prevents overload.

Space Preparation (Physical)

  • Clean: Salt water wash for floor/surfaces. Removes residual energy.
  • Ventilate: Open windows. Moving air = moving energy.
  • Lighting: Single candle at eye level. No harsh overhead lights. Candle = fire element = transformation.
  • Protective Sigils: Draw/place at 4 corners of board area. Salt, chalk, or ink. Standard: Algiz (protection), Othala (boundary), Thuraz (warding).
  • Central Crystal: Clear quartz (amplification/clarity) or Black tourmaline (protection/grounding). At board center.
  • Hardware Timer: 90-minute hard limit. No exceptions. Phone in another room.
  • Grounding Kit (Within Arm's Reach): Coarse salt, 500ml water + electrolytes, protein bar/nuts, LBRP memorized.

Operator Preparation (Physiological)

  • Body: Salt bath or shower (visualize draining). Empty bladder. Light meal 2h prior (not fasted, not full).
  • Mind: 5 min stillness (breath awareness). No phone 30 min prior.
  • Intention Statement (Aloud): "I open this circuit for [specific intent]. I am the operator. I hold the off-switch. I release all outcomes not mine."

The Protective Circle (Optional but Recommended)

  • Salt Circle: Fine salt, continuous line around board + operator(s). No gaps.
  • Protective Sigils (4 Corners): Algiz (protection), Othala (boundary), Thurisaz (warding), Isa (stillness). Salt, chalk, or ink.
  • Central Crystal: Clear quartz (amplification) or Black tourmaline (protection). At board center.
  • Offerings (Optional): Bowl of water (elemental), Salt (purification), Raw meat/bone (life force for entity).

Grounding Kit (Mandatory, Within Arm's Reach)

GROUNDING KIT (Always Within Reach): ===================================== ☐ Coarse salt (pinch in palm = instant ground) ☐ 500ml water + pinch salt (electrolytes) ☐ Protein bar / nuts (blood sugar stabilization) ☐ LBRP / Star Ruby / Gnostic Pentagram MEMORIZED ☐ Hardware timer (90 min max) ☐ Salt bath supplies ready (post-session) ☐ Protein meal ready (post-session) ☐ 8 hours sleep opportunity (post-session)

Phase 2: Calibration — Establishing the Discrimination Baseline

You cannot protect what you cannot distinguish. Calibration establishes your session's discrimination baseline — the ability to distinguish signal (entity) from noise (ideomotor, environmental, psychological).

The 3-Point Calibration (Mandatory, Every Session)

  1. "Show me YES" → Record: speed, pressure, trajectory, pattern, time to first letter.
  2. "Show me NO" → Record: speed, pressure, trajectory, pattern, time to first letter.
  3. "Show me HELLO" → Record entity signature (speed, pressure, trajectory, personality).
CALIBRATION LOG (Every Session): ================================== DATE: ___________ | TIME: ___________ | OPERATOR: ___________ YES: Speed____ Pressure____ Trajectory____ Pattern____ Time____ NO: Speed____ Pressure____ Trajectory____ Pattern____ Time____ HELLO: Speed____ Pressure____ Trajectory____ Pattern____ IDEOMOTOR TEST: Speed____ Pressure____ Trajectory____ Pattern____ ENVIRONMENTAL (60s no hands): Movement? Y/N Speed____ Dir____ Pattern____

The Ideomotor Baseline Test (Mandatory)

  1. Blindfold operator (or close eyes).
  2. Ask a question ONLY YOU know the answer to (e.g., "Is my name [Name]?").
  3. Record the movement signature: speed, pressure, trajectory, pattern.
  4. This = YOUR ideomotor signature. Any movement matching this during session = operator, not external.

Environmental Baseline (Mandatory)

  1. Place planchette on board. NO HANDS.
  2. Wait 60 seconds. Record ANY movement (speed, direction, pattern).
  3. This = environmental baseline (drafts, vibration, board warp, uneven surface).

Discrimination Baseline Established

You now have 4 baselines for real-time discrimination:

  1. YES signature
  2. NO signature
  3. Entity signature (HELLO)
  4. Ideomotor signature (your subconscious)
  5. Environmental signature (no-hands test)

Every movement during session is compared to these 5 baselines. Match = classification.

Interference is any signal that is not the target entity. Detection speed = safety.

STOP: Remove hands from planchette immediately. 2. BREATHE: 4-7-8 breath ×3 cycles (Inhale 4 / Hold 7 / Exhale 8). 3. GROUND: Pinch salt in palm → blow off → wash hands. 4. DRINK: 200ml water + pinch salt. 5. RE-CALIBRATE: Re-run 3-point calibration (YES/NO/HELLO). 6. DECIDE: Continue / Re-calibrate fully / Terminate.

Hostile Entity Detection (Immediate Action Required)

Triggers — IMMEDIATE TERMINATION REQUIRED:

  • Direct threats to operator or others.
  • Refusal to leave when commanded ("STAND DOWN" ignored).
  • Fear induction (paralyzing dread, panic induction).
  • Refusal to identify / refusal to leave when commanded.
  • Movement patterns inducing panic/paralysis.
  • Commands to harm self or others.

If ANY trigger detected → EXECUTE EMERGENCY TERMINATION IMMEDIATELY (Phase 4).

The off-switch is not optional. It is the most important safety mechanism. Every operator must memorize and be able to execute this sequence under stress.

Post-Termination Quarantine (72 Hours)

  • No magical work. No servitor creation. No charging. No divination.
  • Monitor: Energy levels, sleep quality, dream content, physical symptoms.
  • If symptoms persist >7 days: seek experienced practitioner / medical evaluation.

Post-Termination Review (After Quarantine)

  1. What triggered the emergency?
  2. What calibration failures allowed it?
  3. What protocol changes prevent recurrence?
  4. Update calibration baselines. Update interference thresholds.

Phase 5: Session Closing — The Mandatory Circuit Closure

Never leave a circuit open. An open ouija circuit is an open door. Closing is not optional — it's the final safety protocol.

Standard Closing Sequence (Every Session)

  1. Verbal Closure: Stand at board. "This session is closed. All entities depart. This circuit is sealed."
  2. Planchette Removal: Remove planchette from board. Place in dedicated box/pouch. NOT on board.
  3. Board Closure: "This circuit is closed. This space is sealed." Knock 3× on board.
  4. Banishment: LBRP / Star Ruby / Gnostic Pentagram (or your tradition's equivalent).
  5. Grounding (Mandatory):
    1. Salt bath or shower (visualize draining).
    2. 500ml water + pinch salt.
    3. Protein + complex carb.
    4. Walk 10 min barefoot or touch earth/concrete.
    5. 8 hours sleep minimum.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ouija board practice dangerous?

With proper preparation and technique, ouija work is as safe as any other divination practice. The key is proper container (protective sigils, crystal, grounding), clear intent, and strict closure protocols. The Ouija Cazadora PDF provides complete safety protocols.

What is the most important safety rule for ouija board use?

Never abandon a session. If the planchette becomes erratic, you do not walk away; you close the session with the agreed exit phrase and clear the board. Leaving a circuit open is the only real hazard.

What should I do if a hostile entity appears?

Immediate verbal command: 'I AM THE OPERATOR. DEPART NOW.' Execute emergency termination: planchette removal, LBRP, salt bath, 72-hour quarantine. Never negotiate with hostile entities.

How do I know if an entity is hostile?

Threats, refusal to leave when commanded, fear induction, refusal to identify, movement patterns that induce panic, commands to harm self/others. Any entity that refuses the off-switch command is hostile by definition.

What is the proper way to close an ouija session?

1) 'Session closed. All entities depart.' 2) Remove planchette from board. 3) 'Circuit closed.' 4) LBRP/banishment. 5) Grounding: salt bath, water, protein, 8h sleep. 6) Journal log. Never leave board open.
