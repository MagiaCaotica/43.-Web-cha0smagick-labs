Clauneck: Arts, Sciences and the Spirit the Wealth Rituals Misuse

By Frater Alek0s ⬢ September 26, 2026 ⬢ 19 min read

Clauneck is a President of the 72 Spirits, described in the Ars Goetia as an extraordinarily beautiful woman who comes in a great host of thirty legions, and who is said to teach the arts, the sciences and all the crafts. He also makes men invisible, he finds out hidden and secret things, and among those things he finds are the goods that others have stolen. That is the office as the source assigns it. He is not, in that text, a spirit of money. The wealth ritual that carries his name is a much later attachment, and understanding where it came from matters, because the parts of Clauneck that actually work in practice are the parts that have nothing to do with cash.

What the Ars Goetia actually assigns to Clauneck

The description is short and blunt, which is typical of the late-seventeenth-century material the lesser key of Solomon preserves. The rank given is President. The manifestation is a beautiful woman, and the wording in the common printed editions is closer to a very lovely woman than to a beautiful woman, a difference in translation that matters more than it looks. The host is enormous: thirty legions, which puts Clauneck near the top of the book's militarised hierarchy. Nothing in the entry is about money, luck, or commercial success.

What is in the entry is knowledge and access. He is a teacher of arts, sciences and all crafts, in the phrasing that carries the original sense of a full curriculum. He discovers hidden and secret things, treasures in particular. He makes one invisible. He is called excellent at finding the goods of others that have been stolen, and he is credited with the ability to make women love the practitioner. He is said to inspire men and women to boldness, and he can make a person turn aside from a woman, which is the same power read in reverse.

Read as a whole, the entry describes a spirit of competent access. He knows things that are not being said aloud, he can move a person unseen through a world that has decided to see them, and he is associated with the kind of boldness that makes something happen before the moment when it was supposed to become possible. The boldness and the invisibility are the same faculty, pointed in two directions. That is the internal logic of the entry, and it is the reason it holds together as a character rather than a list of powers.

Why a spirit of arts and crafts ended up paying your bills

The wealth association is real and widespread, and it is worth being precise about where it comes from, because the honest answer is that the source does not support it. A President who teaches arts, sciences and all crafts is an obvious candidate for adoption by anyone looking for a spirit of skill, and a spirit of skill in a modern commercial culture is a spirit of money. That inference is cultural, not scriptural. Practitioners in Latin America and Spain in particular have attached Clauneck to abundance work with great enthusiasm, partly through the channel and social channels, partly because a President with thirty legions behind him simply feels powerful when you are looking for a large financial shift.

The older literature is no cleaner about it. The category of demon in the sixteenth century was a catalogue of sins, not a catalogue of professions, and the categories people were already looking for when they read those lists included greed and usury. Mammon is a demon of that list in the straightforward way. Clauneck is not, and the confusion is a later one.

There is a practical reason to keep the association in the room anyway. If a wealth ritual is going to be attempted with a thirty-legion President behind it, Clauneck is a defensible choice on grounds that have nothing to do with wealth. He is associated with boldness, and boldness is the missing ingredient in most failed money workings. The practitioner who has the technique right and the nerve wrong gets a nothing, and nerve is exactly what a President with an enormous host is meant to supply. Treat the money framing as the marketing and the boldness framing as the mechanic.

The numbering problem, and how it should change your reading

The order of the 72 was never settled. The manuscript tradition behind the printed text is inconsistent, and the printed editions are inconsistent with each other, so the same spirit appears at different numbers depending on which book is on your desk. Clauneck sits in the high thirties in the widely circulated arrangement and shifts by one in others. If you are following a ritual instruction that names a number, the number is the least reliable part of the instruction.

The working rule that follows is simple: identify the spirit by its description and by its rank, never by its position. Rank is stable in the source in a way that position is not. President, King, Duke, Marquis, Count, President and Earl, Knight and President are the tiers the book uses, and a spirit keeps its tier across editions. A President who teaches arts and finds hidden things is Clauneck whether your book puts him at thirty-seven or forty.

The second tell is structural. A great many of the 72 have no territorial domain at all. They are described as granters of a faculty, a result, or an effect, and the grantees have to be numerous for that to be a coherent description. Clauneck has no hills, no fields, no building, no office. He has a skill set and a host. If a book you are working from gives him a territory, that book has imported material from elsewhere in the grimoire tradition, which is not a reason to distrust it, but it is a reason to know that you are no longer reading the source.

Arts, sciences and crafts: what the phrase was supposed to cover

The seven liberal arts are recoverable and they are the reason the phrase means what it seems to mean. Grammar, rhetoric, logic, arithmetic, geometry, music and astronomy, taught as a curriculum for a free man rather than a technician, which is why the list is a general education and not an apprenticeship. A grimoire author writing for that reader would reach for exactly this word.

Read as a magic working, the curriculum becomes a diagnostic instrument. You are being handed a way of finding out what you do not know, which is the hidden-things clause again, and the arts are the method rather than the result. The entry does not say he grants skill. It says he teaches it, and a thing taught is a thing you can then do without him. That is the first place where the two clauses of the entry connect, and it is the mechanism you would actually be working with if you took the office seriously.

This is also where the material-tradition reading has something to say. Herbs, roots, stones and resins are the materia medica of that curriculum, and a spirit who teaches the arts sits naturally near the knowledge of what to combine and in what proportion. It is a modern addition, not a source claim, but it is a coherent one and it points at the same thing: Clauneck is about acquiring usable knowledge, not about receiving objects.

The honest objection to all of this is the reification trap. A catalogue entry is a piece of symbolic language, and turning it into a description of an entity with a biography is a move that can be done well and can be done badly. Done well it gives you a working posture. Done badly it gives you a new name for your own desires, and you end up consulting the spirit about your budget instead of doing the budget. The test that catches the bad version is whether the practice changes your behaviour or only your vocabulary.

The invisibility clause is the most useful and the most dangerous

Making oneself unseen is the most quoted power in the entry and the one most often used badly. Read literally it is a request for a capability to move through the world without being observed, and the literal reading leads somewhere that is worth refusing without drama. The common misuse is covert attention on a specific person, and it damages the practitioner more than the target, because it makes the practitioner someone who organises their attention around a target.

Read symbolically the clause is a description of a perceptual state that most people can reach in ordinary life. You have been in a room and read it as a problem about the person you were with. You have been somewhere and stopped noticing yourself, and something happened that you could not afterwards reconstruct. Those are states of not-being-seen in exactly the sense the text is using, and they are far more available than any cloaking device, and they cost nothing.

The constructive version of the clause is about access. Being unseen by a system is what it feels like to understand a rule that other people do not. Being unseen by an audience is what it feels like to do the work before anyone is watching, which is the precondition for most work worth doing. A President who grants that as a faculty is granting a posture, and the posture is achievable by changing what you attend to rather than by changing your appearance.

The line to hold is between anonymity that serves your practice and anonymity that serves your avoidance. The first one keeps you invisible so that you can be wrong in public without cost, which is the entire mechanism of a modern practice. The second one keeps you invisible so that you never have to be found, and it is a different thing wearing the same word. If you cannot say which of the two you are doing, that is the answer.

Thirty legions: the strongest evidence that the entry is about force

Thirty legions is not a modest number. The host sizes in the book range from a few angelic orders to tens of thousands, and at the top end they are read as the spirit's capacity for effect, not as a description of its followers. A President arriving with thirty legions is a spirit that can do something at scale.

The obvious application is boldness, and boldness is the entry's own word. Inspiring men and women to great boldness is a description of a person who moves first, and moving first is what produces most of the outcomes people are looking for when they go looking for a wealth spirit. The failure mode is the opposite of what people expect: not passivity but overreach. A spirit associated with boldness, consulted by someone who is already impulsive, reliably produces the destruction of things the practitioner owned before the financial shift arrived.

The other use of the host is protective. Thirty legions is a quantity, and quantities can be framed as a boundary rather than an attack. The classic form is to build the intent around what the legions stand between rather than what they strike, which converts a force into a perimeter. It is the same image, the same quantity, and the difference between the two uses is entirely in how you write the intent.

It is worth being honest that this is interpretation doing a lot of work. The source gives a number and gives boldness, and the causal link between them is supplied by the practitioner. That is not a flaw in the method. Symbolic systems are made of links supplied by the user, and the discipline is not in finding supplied links but in noticing that you supplied them.

The women's love clause, and the reading the grammar will not support

Clauneck is called excellent at inducing the love of women. That phrase is doing at least two things and it is the most contested in the entry, because practitioners have consistently taken the flattering reading and the grammar does not unambiguously support it. The love women feel for you and your own love of women are different operations, and a text that is this terse does not settle which one is meant.

The two readings behave differently in practice, which is the test that matters. On the first reading, the working concerns your own capacity to relate, and the available material is your own pattern, your own history, your own habit of selecting for a particular kind of person. On the second, it concerns something done to someone else, and the available material is that other person's interior, which is not available and never was. Any practice that requires you to work on another person's desire from the outside is a practice whose results you will not be able to distinguish from coincidence, because you will have no way of checking the inputs.

Take the first reading. It is the one that has content, it is the one that survives contact with a real life, and it is the one where you are the object of the work. Then the clause is genuinely the same clause as the invisibility one, because the invisibility clause is also about a capacity you have rather than an effect you impose. The entry is consistently about what you become able to do, and reading it that way makes all three of its unusual clauses cohere.

The second reading also carries a structural warning. A spirit described as finding the goods of others that have been stolen, alongside a power over other people's desire, is being described with a theft frame. That framing is in the source. Taking the theft frame seriously means declining the parts of the entry that treat other people's interior as raw material, and it costs nothing, because those parts never worked.

Working with a President: the practical form

Because he is a President and not a servant, the traditional form of the working is a request rather than a construction, and getting that backwards is the most common error. A servant is built to a specification and carries out instructions. A President is approached, and the difference is not a formality. A construction that treats a President as a servant produces something that behaves like a servant, which means the specificity of the office has been wasted, and you are running a routine autonomous process when what you wanted was a competence you could consult.

The practical sequence is short. Define the knowledge you actually lack, in writing, in one sentence, with the specific gap named. That gap has to be a real one, because a President given a vague request returns a vague result and no reader can tell whether the vagueness came from the spirit or from the question. Then state the access you want, not the outcome you want. A President of arts and sciences is better asked to see the structure of a problem than to produce a particular number.

Write the request as a question you could not answer yourself. That constraint is what makes it a consultation rather than a request for a servant, and it also keeps the work honest, because a question you could have answered yourself is one where you will not be able to evaluate the answer. Finally, decide in advance what you will do differently depending on what you get back, and write that down before you start. A President consulted with no consequence attached is a reading, and a reading is fine, but it should be named as one.

The response in practice tends to come as a change in what you notice, not as information. The structure of the problem appears as a thing you can now see the shape of, and the working is finished when the shape is visible. Expecting a delivered fact produces a disappointment that gets misread as failure of the spirit rather than as a misframed request.

How this differs from the money spirits

The money spirits work through a different grammar, and mixing the two is a common way to get a nothing that reads as a failure. A financial working of the Mammon type is a transaction. Something is asked for, conditions are named, and the conditions are what make the structure hold. There is a stake, there is a review, and the review is the part that does the work. Clauneck is not in that grammar, and the thirty legions do not help.

A transaction asks for an effect. A competence asks for an access. If you bring a transaction frame to Clauneck you will get a President-shaped version of a transaction, which in practice means an outcome you receive without knowing which of your actions caused it, and the most common result is a spending increase with no income behind it. That outcome is not a spiritual failure. It is a competently answered question you did not ask.

Where the two can be joined honestly is in sequence rather than in fusion. Establish the access first, through a knowledge question, until you can see the actual structure of your situation. Then, separately, run a properly constructed transaction for the specific financial move, with the conditions written down. Two workings, two structures, run in the right order, is a better plan than one working that tries to be both and ends up being neither.

There is a reason to run the sequence in that order specifically. The access working changes what you can see about your own position, and most people running a money working have not done that part, so the transaction gets written from a picture that is out of date. The competence comes first not because it is spiritually superior but because it is chronologically prior.

Signs that the working is landing, and signs that it is not

The positive signs for a competence working are all behavioural, because that is what competences do. You learn a piece of the material you did not have. You become able to name something you could previously only feel. Your decisions on the relevant question get faster, because you stop re-deriving the same ground every time. None of these are dramatic and all of them are checkable, which is what makes them useful.

There is one more sign worth naming because it is specific to working with a President. The host gets invoked in the ordinary sense. Thirty legions is an enormous quantity and a large quantity produces an increase in scope, so the working often shows up as noticing that the question you asked has four parts instead of one. That is not a sign that the spirit got distracted. It is the host doing the one thing the entry gives it a large amount of.

The negative signs are equally behavioural. The clearest one is vocabulary inflation without behaviour change, which is what reification looks like from the inside: you have a much richer vocabulary for talking about your situation and identical behaviour. The second is a loss of privacy that you did not choose, which is a sign you have taken the invisibility clause in the wrong direction. The third is escalating effort, where each working needs to be bigger than the last to produce the same effect, which usually means the effect was never the point.

The fourth is the one that ends a working: the practice has started to cost you things that have nothing to do with it. Sleep, money, work, relationships. A working that takes those is not a strong working. If the practice has started to require the sacrifice of ordinary capacity, stop and treat the loss as the result.

Five failure modes, named

The first is the category error. Clauneck consulted as a money spirit produces a transaction that was never properly constructed, and the honest description of what happens is that a competence was asked a question about a number and answered the question it was actually given. The symptom is a financial shift with no traceable cause in your own conduct. The fix is not more effort with the same framing. It is separating the two workings.

The second is position-based selection, which comes straight from trusting the number over the description. The symptom is a working that produces the behaviour of a completely different spirit, usually a minor or a lesser demon, and the practitioner experiences it as a weak Clauneck when what has actually happened is a misidentification. The fix is to re-read the entry for rank and description and to stop consulting by number entirely.

The third is the theft frame taken literally, which shows up as a growing fascination with what other people have and a shrinking interest in the actual post. The entry does contain that framing, which is why the risk is real rather than imagined. The fix is to name the clause you are declining, in writing, and to run the working on the hidden-things and boldness clauses where the material is actually usable.

The fourth is budget-shaped invisibility. A practice that is generating a need for secrecy about itself, in a household, at work, or with a partner, has crossed from access into avoidance, and it will present as progress for a while. The fix is to bring one other person in, deliberately, and to say plainly what the practice is and what it is for. That single act resolves most of what follows.

The fifth is the quiet one: the working becomes a way of not deciding. Consultation is genuinely useful for seeing structure, and it is also a very good-looking substitute for choosing. If you notice that you consult more often and act no faster, the consultation has become the practice's own reward, and the entry's competence has been repurposed into delay.

Ethics: a benign entry with a real cost

Clauneck is among the more benign of the 72 in the source. Nothing in the entry is a curse, a blight, a disease or a destruction, and the two powers that could be aimed outward, invisibility and the influence of women, are both better read as capacities than as attacks. There is nothing in this office that requires you to harm anyone.

The costs are real and they are not all symbolic. The liberal arts clause leads toward materia medica, and materia medica means substances with actual dose limits and actual interactions. A working that involves herbs, roots or resins is a working with a pharmacology attached, and the responsible version of that is the same as it would be anywhere else: known substances, known quantities, no stacking, and a willingness to conclude that a substance is not for you.

The other cost is the one the entry's theft frame makes visible. The gifts on offer are other people's attention and other people's property, in the sense that a President of arts and sciences confers access to what other people know. A practitioner who uses that access to advantage people who are relying on your discretion has not done anything the source forbids, and has still lost the thing that made the practice worth doing.

The discipline that keeps this clean is small and easy to state. Keep the working pointed at your own capacity, where the source's actual power is, and let the results in the world be a side effect rather than the target. That is both the more effective version and the more defensible one, and it is a coincidence worth having rather than a compromise.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Clauneck a demon of money and wealth?

The Ars Goetia does not make him one. His entry assigns him arts, sciences, all crafts, invisibility, and the discovery of hidden and secret things, with a reference to goods stolen by others. The wealth association is a modern adoption, culturally explained by the fact that a spirit of skill in a commercial culture gets read as a spirit of money. Practising him as a money spirit is possible but it is a later framing, and a competence working is a different grammar than a transaction.

What is the difference between a President and a Duke in the 72?

Rank is a tier of authority, and it is the most stable identifying feature in the book because the numbering of the spirits shifts between editions while the tiers do not. A President is above the nobility of the book. The practical difference shows up in how you work: a Duke and below can be built into a servant or a grimoire-style operation, while a President is approached as a consulted authority. Treating a President as a servitor produces something that behaves like a servitor, which wastes the office.

What does making one invisible actually mean in practice?

Read symbolically it is the state of not being observed, which most people can reach: reading a room as a problem about a person, or losing track of yourself and then being unable to reconstruct what happened. Read constructively it is about access, being unseen by a system or by an audience so that you can understand a rule other people cannot, or do work before anyone is watching. The literal reading, using it to observe a specific person covertly, is the misuse to avoid.

How do I work with a spirit who is a President?

With a request, not a construction. Write down the specific knowledge you lack in one sentence, state the access you want rather than the outcome you want, and frame it as a question you could not answer yourself. Then decide in advance what you will do differently depending on the answer, and write that down first. The response generally arrives as a change in what you notice, the structure of a problem becoming visible, rather than as a delivered fact.

Can Clauneck be used to find hidden objects or lost property?

The discovery of hidden and secret things is the cleanest part of the entry to work with, and it applies to lost property, to the structure of a situation you cannot see into, and to your own patterns. The theft frame in the same clause should be declined explicitly rather than ignored, and the question should stay pointed at your own access to information rather than at another person's intentions.
