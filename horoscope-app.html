The subscription problem with horoscope apps

A subscription horoscope app is not selling astrology. It is selling notifications. Once a day the app buzzes, you open it, you read three lines, and it has cost you the price of a coffee every week for a text that could have come from a list of twelve sentences.

What people are actually looking for when they search for a horoscope app is not predictions. It is timing: what phase the moon is in, whether it is void of course, whether a planet is retrograde, when the planetary hours fall. That information is exact, it is free, and it belongs to a calculator rather than to a subscription.

What to use instead, free in the browser

Six tools cover the entire daily layer:

  • Lunar phase — current phase, illumination and sign.

  • Moon VOC — whether the moon is void of course right now.

None of them needs an account, none of them sends a notification, and none of them will ask you for a card.
